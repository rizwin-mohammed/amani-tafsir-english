### Section – 18

*[Verse 116]*

A verse that states with certainty that Allah will not forgive shirk has likewise passed before in this very surah, too, in the 48th verse. There we have also given the *[p. 1114]* necessary explanation. Therefore, there is no need to describe it repeatedly here. But what was stated further in the 48th verse about associating partners with Allah was that he has fabricated a huge sin ( **فَقَدِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰٓ إِثۡمًا عَظِيمًا** ). Here what has been said is that he has strayed, a far-off error ( **فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ ضَلَٰلَۢا بَعِيدًا** ). It is in a context that speaks referring to the fabrication, and the altering, of the People of the Scripture that that verse stands there. Here it is following the description of the incident of Ibn Ubairiq that this verse is. That may be the reason for saying it in that way there, and in this way here. **الله اعلم** Closing sentences in keeping with the context are the custom of the Quran, are they not. Allah continues:-

*[Verses 117–121]*

*[p. 1116]* The chief among the kinds of shirk is worshipping those other than Allah, is it not. And the essence and life of acts of worship, for their part, is supplication. That the Prophet ﷺ has declared ‘Supplication is itself worship’, and ‘Supplication is the marrow of worship’, is also well known. Allah points out that the polytheists do not call upon in supplication any but some females, and the insolent Satan. Not only of the mushriks in Arabia at that time, but of all the idol-worshipping polytheists who exist today, too, the state is more or less this very one. Most of the tribes among the Arabs had some deities of their own, and idols set up in their names. Today too, likewise, for the people of some lands, and for some castes - even for some families - special deities and idols of their own exist. The Arabs used to speak of their deities as angels. That is, their conception was that their idols were the installed images of the angels. Even idol-names such as ‘Lat, 'Uzza, Manat’ they used as female names. They also used to be in the habit of saying about the idol of a particular tribe **انثى بنى فلان** (the female of such-and-such a family). Many of the idol-worshippers of today do not, like the Arabs, say about idols that they are angels, or say about the angels that they are the daughters of Allah. At the same time, in place of ‘angels’, usages and conceptions such as ‘deity, goddess, god, divine incarnation’ can be seen in all of them ‘They do not call upon in supplication, besides Allah, any but females, ( **إِن يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ إِنَٰثٗا** ) - a riwayah from Ibn 'Abbas (r) showing that what is meant by **إِنَٰثٗا** (females) in that sentence is lifeless things, and a riwayah from Hasan (r) showing that it is every thing without life - be it a dry piece of wood or a stone -, have been quoted. ( **كما فى ابن كثير** )

‘The insolent Satan ( **شَيۡطَٰنٗا مَّرِيدٗا** )’ - that what was said here is meant of Iblis is clear from the statements that follow. As can be seen in places such as Su: Al-A'raf 16; Al-Hijr 39; Sad 82, when, following his not consenting to do sujud to the Prophet Adam (a), he became the object of the curse and wrath of Allah, Iblis said, swearing an oath, that he would try to lead the children of Adam astray in whatever way he could. It is some parts of those oaths that Allah quotes here in the 118th and 119th verses. But it may be said that no one calls upon Iblis in supplication, does he. It is by yielding to his evil incantations, and evil counsels, that all the mushriks have been worshipping, and calling upon in supplication, their own gods - whether they be idols, or stars, or *[p. 1117]* stones, or great souls, or goddesses and gods, or anything else -. That is the purport of having said that they do not call upon in supplication any but Satan - Iblis -. Otherwise, the intent is not that worship is performed before Iblis too, as worship is performed before idols. And there are not lacking, either, some who worship the satans themselves without an idol or an installed image. (About this, see what has been stated in the explanation of the verses Su: Saba' 41, and Ya-Sin 60.)

Since the incitement behind all kinds of shirks and sins is from Iblis, Allah has specially pointed out what his real position is. After he has been qualified as being insolent ( **مَّرِيدٗا** ), and as Allah having cursed him ( **لَّعَنَهُ ٱللَّهُۘ** ), a reminder is given that he is the lifelong enemy of mankind, and one who has taken a vow to lead them astray - quoting his own words indeed -. Following that, the consequences that come about if one is caught in his deception have also been pointed out. Very many Quran verses and sayings of the Prophet that give warning concerning Iblis and the satans who are of his kind can be seen. Very strong, very extensive, very clever, and along with that invisible, is the harm of Satan among human beings. That is the reason for it. Look at what he says, insolently addressing Allah:-

(1) **لَأَتَّخِذَنَّ مِنۡ عِبَادِكَ** ...... (I shall indeed make and keep a fixed share from Your servants.) That is, the gist is that I shall indeed bring about from among human beings, against You, a section as my followers who stand at my beck and call. It need not be said that the greater part of human beings are ones who have been deceived, in one way if not in another, by the evil promptings of that accursed one. Iblis has openly said ‘Except a few, I shall throw the rest of the children of Adam into disarray.’ (17 : 62), and ‘I shall lead astray all of those who are not Your servants free from taint.’ (38 : 82).

(2) **وَلَأُضِلَّنَّهُمۡ وَلَأُمَنِّيَنَّهُمۡ** ..... *[TN: the print has these two words in the reverse order, وَلَأُمَنِّيَنَّهُمۡ وَلَأُضِلَّنَّهُمۡ; the author's Malayalam rendering that follows has the order of the verse.]* (I shall indeed lead them astray, and delude them.) ‘I shall take my seat for them on Your straight path, and then I shall come to them through their front, and through their back, and through their right side, and through their left side; and You will not find most of them grateful.’ (7:16, 17), and ‘On the earth I shall make adornment for them.’ (15 : 39), and so on - likewise he has openly said many other things too. That the leading astray, and the deluding, of Satan are all indeed - according to the difference of individuals, and of the circumstances of time and place - varied, can be understood from the next sentences themselves.

*[p. 1118]* (3) **وَلَأٓمُرَنَّهُمۡ فَلَيُبَتِّكُنَّ ءَاذَانَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ** ..... (I shall command them, and so they will indeed slit and cut the ears of the cattle.) That is, the gist is that I shall suggest many things to them, and that, according to that, they will keep doing wanton acts such as slitting and cutting the ears of the cattle. Setting apart goats, cattle and camels as votive offerings in the names of idols has gone on being the custom of the mushriks, has it not. In certain special kinds of offerings, slitting and cutting the ear of camels as its sign was also their custom. It is that which Allah has mentioned specifically here as an example.

(4) **وَلَأٓمُرَنَّهُمۡ فَلَيُغَيِّرُنَّ خَلۡقَ ٱللَّهِۚ** ... (I shall command them, and so they will also alter the creation of Allah.) This is a word of a somewhat wide meaning. It can be said in general that all the practices that attempt to bring about changes and alterations in the natures that Allah has determined, and that disfigure them, are included in this. In this age, when religious teachings and moral values are not usually given value, examples of this can be seen among human beings in abundance indeed. That everything such as homosexual intercourse, sterilisation, women adopting the dress of men and men the dress of women, tattooing on the body, making an artificial appearance for the body by means of artificial dyes and devices, are examples of this can be understood from the sayings of the Prophet, and so on.

After describing, through his own words indeed, what kind Satan is, Allah has also pointed out the consequences of adopting him as the manager of affairs by way of following the counsels of that Satan, without adopting Allah as the manager of affairs. Yes, that it is a cause of irreparable loss, that his promises will be utterly hollow and deception, that what he does is only to delude in matters that are not attainable, that for those who have been deceived by it the eternal punishment of the Fire will be the support *[DOUBT: ആധാരം is printed clearly; it usually means "basis, support"; beside the verse's مَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ ("their resort") it may be meant as "abode, refuge".]*, and so on.

*[Verse 122]*

*[p. 1119]* The gains of those who, without making Satan their manager, accept Allah alone as manager and adopt true faith and good deeds, must be the very opposite. Yes, if for them it is the eternal punishment of the Fire, for these it is an eternal and fully happy heavenly life that will be obtained. If all the promises that Satan, whom they made their manager of affairs, gave them were lies and artifice, all the promises that Allah, whom these adopted as manager of affairs, gave to them will be wholly true and real.

*[Verses 123–124]*

*[p. 1120]* Addressing the true believers, Allah makes known in these verses a principle of fundamental importance. Success and salvation are not the monopoly right of a community or of a section; nor are they obtained by desire or by claim. It is by true faith and good deeds in accordance with the legal directions of Allah and the teachings of the Rasuls that it is attained. In it there is not the distinction of male and female, either. What matters is who did good, who did evil. Whatever is done that is evil, its doer will experience its fruit. Whatever is done that is good, its doer too will experience its fruit without any shortfall. But only the good that is done with true faith will be acceptable with Allah. This is that principle.

That the good deeds of those who do not adopt true faith - even if they be good deeds when looked at in themselves - are not acceptable with Allah, the Quran has made clear many a time. Without saying that those who do good deeds, whether male or female, will enter Paradise, Allah has made that matter clear here too, saying **وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ** (if he does them being a true believer). Not only that, it is known from the Quran and from hadith that, besides believing in Allah, it is only for good deeds that are done intending His pleasure and recompense that recompense will be given in the Hereafter, and that for deeds that are done aiming at something worldly no recompense will be obtained. Explaining the verse **فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ** (If a person does an atom's weight of good, he will see it), some are wont to say that true faith is not a condition for good deeds being accepted, and that whoever does a good deed, whether he be a believer or an unbeliever, it will not fail to obtain recompense. It can be said decisively that this is not right, and that it is contrary to many clear statements of the Quran.

It has come in some riwayahs that a debate took place between the Muslims and the People of the Scripture, that it is we who are entitled to salvation and success more than you, and that it was following that that these verses were revealed.
That some Sahabis, when this verse was revealed, became afraid, saying such things as: If all evils are indeed going to have their respective recompense experienced, how shall we *[p. 1121]* be saved? Who will there be who has not done evil at all?, and that the Prophet ﷺ comforted them, saying that for all difficulties indeed, such as illness, grief, that may befall the true believers - in case they keep patience - good fruits will keep being obtained for them, can be seen in more than one hadith that Ahmad, Muslim, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i (r) and others have recorded. (Recall what has been described in the explanation of the 49th verse concerning the meaning and use of the word **نَقِيرٗا**.)

*[Verses 125–126]*

*[p. 1122]* It can be said that this is the practical form, and the explanation, of what was said in the previous verse, doing good deeds being a true believer. To submit to Him completely, worshipping Him alone according to the commands and directions of Allah; to do good deeds sincerely, without any admixtures touching them. Thus to follow the very same practice that Ibrahim (a) had been adopting; there is no other religious practice better than this. Whoever adopts that way, it is they who are the real successful ones and fortunate ones - that is the purport.

The Prophet Ibrahim (a) is, is he not, the person whom the Arabs, including the Muslims and the mushriks, and the People of the Scripture, comprising the Jews and the Christians, have been honouring and respecting with pride, as their ancestral father, and as a special dignitary in the family of the prophets. And everyone claims that it is they who follow the way of the Prophet Ibrahim (a). That is why it was said, indicating that the way he adopted is such-and-such, that those who followed it are the ones of the best religious practice. Allah alerts that not only is he a great man whom all accept, but Allah has also adopted him as an intimate friend - as one most beloved and who has attained very great nearness -, and that therefore each party ought to try to make real the claim that it is on his way that they stand. But Allah has also pointed out what the basic reason is for commanding that all should submit completely to Allah. Yes, all that is in the heavens and the earth is His. All indeed is His creation. All is in His ownership. All are His slaves, too. Whatever - wherever - however it exists, He is also One who knows all that fully and minutely.
