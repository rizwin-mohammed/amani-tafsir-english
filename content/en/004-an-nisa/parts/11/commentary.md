*[Verses 47–48]*

*[p. 1024]* By **مَا نَزَّلۡنَا** (what We have sent down) what is meant is the Quran. The import of its having been described that the Quran is one that confirms the earlier Scriptures as true has been explained in the commentary of Al-Baqarah 41, and of Ali 'Imran : 2 *[TN: in the verse numbering of the Quran text used on this site, the verse that describes the Book as confirming what was before it (مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ) is 3:3, and this translation's Ali 'Imran explains it under verse 3.]*. Allah warns the Jews that, in case they do not believe in the Quran, He may wipe off your faces from the front side and make them to the back side, or else curse you in the manner that He cursed the people of the shabbat; and that it is good not to give occasion for that; and that whatever matter Allah intends to bring into effect will indeed come into effect. The information about the people of the shabbat, and of His having transformed them by making them apes, has gone before in Al-Baqarah : 65. In A'raf 163 - 166 further information is also coming.

The gist of this warning will be that if He wishes, Allah is able to make you experience one of these two things in this very life; you know, surely, what happened to the people of the shabbat. Or else, it can also be the gist that you must remember that He will make you experience in this world the experience that the people of the shabbat had in this world, and will punish you on the Day of Qiyamah by turning your face backwards. Imam Razi (r) has said with certainty that the turning backwards of the face is an event that is going to happen on the Day of Qiyamah. He has also pointed out some reasons for that. The opinion of some commentators is that it is a usage in a figurative form. In case you do not believe, Allah will bring you to a state more disgraceful than today's, or, He will compel you to flee, packing you off from the Hijaz where you now live, to Syria, your old place - it will then be a hint of that, that is in it. **الله اعلم**

Following on, Allah has made clear that Allah will not forgive shirk, and that any sins below it He will forgive to whom He wills. In the essence of Allah, or in His actions *[p. 1025]* or in His rights of authority, or in His special attributes, to ascribe a share or equality to anyone else is shirk. That Allah will not forgive shirk is what the Quran and the Hadith have repeatedly proclaimed unambiguously. It can be seen that Allah has repeatedly said these same words in the 116th verse below also. As far as the Jews are concerned, although they are not ordinarily known by the name mushriks, many matters of the nature of shirk are prevalent among them. Two matters the Quran has stated clearly about them. Their claim that Uzair (a) is the son of Allah, and their having set up their priest-scholars as Rabbs, or, their having made their rulings and prohibitions equal to the rulings and prohibitions of Allah. As for the Christians, it is that Isa (a) is the son of Allah that they have accepted as the basic principle of their religion. If one speaks about the Muslim community, it has become difficult even to say how many are the superstitions and evil customs of the nature of shirk that have crept in among them in the course of time. Refuge is in Allah!

Allah makes known that Allah may forgive the sins that are not shirk - however huge they may be -. The forgiving of sin is only a generosity of His. It is not a duty of His. It is for that reason that it has been commanded to repent and turn back from sins, and to pray for the said generosity - for the forgiveness of sins -. Allah, surely, knows who all ought to be forgiven, who are the ones that have a right to it, and so on. It is for that reason that, without stopping at saying in general that He will forgive the sins that are not shirk, He has also said to whom He wills (**لِمَن يَشَآءُ**). As far as shirk is concerned, other than by desisting from it and turning back to tawhid - by having repented or by having sought forgiveness of sins - there is no deliverance from it. If one dies in shirk, it is the punishment of Hell for all time that will be experienced. Those who die in tawhid and are sinners - in case they do not become deserving of the forgiveness of sins from Allah - will have to experience punishments according to their sins, and after that they will be given entry into Paradise. All this is what has been known clearly from the Quran and from the sayings of the Prophet.

The gist of a famous hadith recorded from Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (r) is like this:- Any servant who said **لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ** (there is none worthy of worship except Allah) and then died in that same manner, he will not fail to enter Paradise. so the Prophet ﷺ said to me. I asked: ‘Even if he commits adultery and steals?’ The noble one said:’ (Yes,) even if he commits adultery and steals.’ I asked the same question again. The noble one repeated the same answer. The fourth time the noble one also said: ‘Even if it is displeasing to Abu Dharr, (it is so indeed.)’.’ Bukhari, Muslim (r) and the other hadith scholars have all recorded this *[p. 1026]* hadith. Many other hadiths too that indicate this idea can be seen. It has also been narrated that whenever he quoted this hadith, he used to also say that word that the Messenger ﷺ said - the word ‘Even if it is displeasing to Abu Dharr’.

A reality needs to be pointed out here. That if one says with the tongue or knows the meaning of the sentence **لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ**, then one may do any wrong and offence, and that Paradise is fully certain, and that one will not be punished, and so on - in the name of some hadiths like this - some people who are superstitious and fools are wont to suppose and to interpret. Exploiting that, some people of vested interest who deny the authoritativeness of Hadith are also wont to portray such hadiths as contemptible and ridiculous. It can be said decisively that both of these are sheer falsehood and contrary to fact. What has been said here is clear from that very word that the Prophet ﷺ said, ‘If one says ‘La ilaha illallahu’ and then dies in that same manner’ (**قَالَ لاَ إلَهَ الاالله ثُمَّ مَاتَ عَلَى ذَلِكَ**). Its meaning, surely, is: if one believes in tawhid and dies in tawhid itself. The Prophet ﷺ has not said that that servant will not enter Hell at all, or that there will be no punishment for him. He has only said that he will enter Paradise (**دَخَلَ الجَنَّةَ**). The gist of what was said is that, in case one does not commit shirk, after the punishment of the other sins is over, one will afterwards get deliverance, and will receive entry into Paradise. This is also what has been known more clearly from other hadiths. At the same time, the reality that as sins increase - in case one does not repent and turn back from them - it will keep causing loss to iman (true faith), and will finally become a cause for it to perish completely, is also what has been known from the Quran, and from the Hadith.

*[Verses 49–50]*

*[p. 1027]* The thin film that wraps round the date-stone and is like a dry onion skin is called **قِطۡمِير** (qitmir), and the hollow that can be seen like a dot on its back part is called **نَقِير** (naqir), and the thin fibre-like strand that lies in the groove that is on its belly part is called **فَتِيل** (fatil). In the sense of very trifling - just as it is wont to be said in Malayalam ‘a whit's measure, a mustard-seed's measure’ and so on - these three words are used in Arabic. In the Quran the first has been used in Su: Fatir 13, the second in 53 of this surah and elsewhere, and the third in this verse and elsewhere, in that same sense.

It is about the self-praises of the People of the Scripture, such as: we are the children and the beloved ones of Allah, none but we will enter Paradise, we are sinless, that it is stating. No one will become pure by praising oneself that one is pure. Only those whom Allah has made pure will become pure. He, for His part, will not show injustice or partiality to anyone. He knows who are deserving of being made pure and who are not. What He will do is to act according to that - is the gist. Self-praise is by its very nature a wrong. When, along with that, it is also by falsely asserting in the name of Allah what is not so, it will, surely, be extremely severe and full of insolence. That is what the sentence **وَكَفَىٰ بِهِۦٓ إِثۡمٗا مُّبِينًا** (that itself is enough as a clear offence!) points out.
