## Corrections made (before → after)

### Sentence structure

1. p. 1024 "ക്വുർആനിൽ വിശ്വസിക്കാത്ത പക്ഷം, നിങ്ങളുടെ മുഖങ്ങൾ …": no subject is printed in the condition. "in case they do not believe in the Quran, He may wipe off your faces" → "in case of not believing in the Quran, He may wipe off your faces".
2. p. 1024 "നിങ്ങൾ വിശ്വസിക്കാത്തപക്ഷം, … എത്തിക്കുമെന്നോ, … നിർബ്ബന്ധിതരാക്കുമെന്നോ ഉള്ള സൂചനയായിരിക്കും അപ്പോൾ അതിലുള്ളത്.": "In case you do not believe, Allah will bring you to a state more disgraceful than today's, or, He will compel you to flee, packing you off from the Hijaz where you now live, to Syria, your old place - it will then be a hint of that, that is in it." → "What is in it will then be a hint that, in case you do not believe, Allah will bring you to a state more disgraceful than today's, or that, packing you off from the Hijaz where you now live, He will compel you to flee to Syria, your old place." (The English read as two statements of fact; the Malayalam is one sentence saying both are what the words hint at. No dash is printed.)
3. p. 1025 "അതിൽനിന്നു വിരമിച്ചു തൗഹീദിലേക്ക് മടങ്ങുന്നത്കൊണ്ടല്ലാതെ ഖേദിച്ചതു കൊണ്ടോ പാപമോചനം തേടിയതുകൊണ്ടോ - അതിൽ നിന്നു രക്ഷയില്ല.": only the closing dash is printed. "other than by desisting from it and turning back to tawhid - by having repented or by having sought forgiveness of sins - there is no deliverance from it" → "other than by desisting from it and turning back to tawhid, by having repented or by having sought forgiveness of sins - there is no deliverance from it". The added opening dash made repenting and seeking forgiveness read as the way of turning back to tawhid; the author's point is that they do not deliver from shirk except by leaving it for tawhid.

## Open doubts for the reviewer

- **p. 1028 جِبَتٌ** (inline DOUBT): at 600 dpi the mark over ب looks like a fatha, with tanwin damm on ت; the text layer has a sukun (U+0652) there. The word is jibt (جِبۡت), with sukun. Given as printed; the reviewer may prefer جِبْتٌ.
- **v. 56 حَكِيمٗا "അഗാധജ്ഞൻ, യുക്തിജ്ഞാനി"** is rendered "the Profoundly Knowing, the Sagacious"; earlier tables give "the Sagacious" for യുക്തിമാൻ. യുക്തിജ്ഞാനി ("one who knows the wisdom of things") is a different word; left, the reviewer may prefer "the One who knows the wisdom (of things)" to keep them apart.
- **p. 1028 ആഭിചാരം** is "sorcery" (as Al-Baqarah 2:102 "sihr, sorcery, witchcraft"); An-Nisa' part 07 has "sihr (black magic)". Left.
- v. 53 "അവർ മനുഷ്യർക്ക് നൽകുകയില്ല" after "[ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നെങ്കിൽ]" is rendered literally "they will not give" (not "would not"). Left.
