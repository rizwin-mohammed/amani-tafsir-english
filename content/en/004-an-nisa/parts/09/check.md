## Open doubts for the reviewer

- No inline DOUBT markers in this part; none was needed.
- **Two Malayalam words with near-identical English.** v. 36 فَخُورًا ദുരഭിമാനി is rendered "conceited" (panel and table), and p. 1008 അഹംഭാവം (in the Ibn Mas'ud hadith, three times) is rendered "conceit". Earlier word tables render അഹംഭാവം കാണിക്കുക "show arrogance" (Al-Baqarah), but here അഹങ്കാരം "arrogance" stands beside it in the same passage, so "conceit" keeps the two apart. Left; the reviewer may prefer "self-importance" or "pride" for അഹംഭാവം, or "vainglorious" for ദുരഭിമാനി.
- p. 1012 "എനി, ഇപ്പോൾ മതി!" is rendered "Then, it is enough now!"; എനി is taken as ഇനി ("now, from now on"). Left.
