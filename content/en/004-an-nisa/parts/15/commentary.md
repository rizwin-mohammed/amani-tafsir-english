### Section – 10

Allah has described in the previous verses very many legal directions, and counsels, in many religious, and personal, and social spheres. In the verses that follow, He describes many matters that the true believers ought to know in confronting the enemies who create obstruction to the existence and the propagation of Islam, and so on. Allah says:-

*[Verse 71]*

*[p. 1054]* The true believers must always be vigilant about the enemies. That is, they must keep remaining ready, preparing men and equipment so as to confront whenever need arises, and must be on guard against the attacks that may come from them unexpectedly. The gist is that setting out against them, and clashing with them, may be band by band, or all joining together at once, according to the occasion.

*[Verses 72–73]*

*[p. 1055]* It is about hypocrites like 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy, who were living on in the midst of the Muslims, that it states. Slipping away at the opportune moment without taking part in battles, and trying to turn others back, was their custom. If defeat, or hardships, befell the Muslims in some clashes, they would argue that their not going along with them was a great favour that Allah had indeed done for them. On the contrary, if it happened that victory came to the Muslims, or that they obtained ghanimah or the like, they would also lament at not having been able to take part along with them and become sharers in those gains. If one sees this, it will seem that there was no relationship at all between them and the Muslims.

*[Verses 74–75]*

*[p. 1057]* It is because they keep aiming at worldly comforts that there is cause for the hypocrites to adopt the stand stated in the previous verse. But, those who aim at otherworldly gains, and are ready to give up worldly comforts for that, need not hesitate at all to fight in the way of Allah. Whether they are killed in battle, or achieve victory - whichever of the two it is, all right - Allah will indeed give them a huge reward: that indeed is the import of the first verse. Why should you hesitate to fight in the matter of upholding the din of Allah, and in the matter of the weak men and women and children who keep grieving, without a way to escape from the wrongdoing and persecutions of the enemies! Are you not the ones who ought to come forward of yourselves for it? The second verse is one that thus gives the true believers zeal for battle and inspiration.

There were a number of people in Makkah who had accepted true faith, and who had become victims of the wrongdoing and persecutions of the mushriks on account of it. The Quraish did not allow them to come away to Madinah in hijrah, or to abandon the country and go. It is about them that the weak ones are spoken of here. What Allah has quoted are the prayers that they, who were helpless and worn out, kept making with their hearts aching, so that deliverance would be given to them. Indeed without delaying very much at all, Allah also answered that prayer. With the conquest of Makkah, it became possible for them to lead a peaceful life freely in Makkah itself.

*[Verse 76]*

*[p. 1058]* The gist is that, however much support and majesty the party of Satan, who is the enemy of Allah, may have, it is to the party of Allah that victory and strength will come in the end. About the meanings of Taghut(**طاغوت**), it has already been explained before. It has also been pointed out before that what is meant is all evil deities, including the devils and the worshipped things.
