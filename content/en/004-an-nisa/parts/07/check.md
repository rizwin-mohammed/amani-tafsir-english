## Corrections made (before → after)

### Consistency with part 06

1. വേശ്യാവൃത്തർ (v. 25 panel and table): "who are not harlots" → "who are not those who practise harlotry" (part 06 p. 983 rendered വേശ്യാവൃത്തർ "those who practise harlotry").
2. p. 990 stray stop: the `[TN: …]` was removed and the stop mirrored: "their evil promptings. and provocations." (as earlier parts).
3. p. 987 പതിവ്രതകളായ സ്ത്രീകൾ: "women of wifely chastity" → "women who are chaste wives" (part 06 table: പതിവ്രതകൾ "chaste wives"; പാതിവ്രത്യം stays "wifely chastity").

### Rendering

4. v. 25 panel "ചാരിത്ര്യ ശുദ്ധകളായ [സ്വതന്ത്ര] സ്ത്രീകളുടെ": "upon women [free women] pure of chastity" → "upon [free] women pure of chastity" (the bracket holds only സ്വതന്ത്ര).
5. അഭിപ്രായ സ്വാതന്ത്ര്യം (pp. 991–992, three times): "freedom of choice" → "freedom of opinion" (literal; സ്വാതന്ത്ര്യം alone stays "freedom"). "a dealing with freedom of choice" → "a dealing of freedom of opinion" (സ്വാതന്ത്ര്യത്തിന്റെ ഇടപാട്).
6. p. 992 hadith "ഒരാൾ ഇടപാട് ഒഴിഞ്ഞു കളയുമെന്നു ഭയന്ന് മറ്റേയാൾ അവനെ (വേഗം) വിട്ടുപിരിയുവാൻ പാടുള്ളതല്ല": "It is not allowed for one to separate from the other (quickly) fearing that he will withdraw from the dealing." → "It is not allowed for the other one, fearing that one will withdraw from the dealing, to separate from him (quickly)." (who fears and who leaves, as printed).
