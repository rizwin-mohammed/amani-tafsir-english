## Corrections made (before → after)

### Arabic as printed

1. p. 975, printed فَاحِشَةٍ (kasr tanwin): **فَٰحِشَةٗ** → **فَٰحِشَةٖ** (the Uthmani spelling with the printed ending, as part 05 did on p. 970).
2. p. 981, printed اِحْصَان / اِحْصَانٌ (plain alif with kasrah, 300 dpi): **إِحْصَان** → **اِحْصَان**, **إِحْصَانٌ** → **اِحْصَانٌ**.
3. p. 985, printed مَّا وَرَاء هُنَّ (600 dpi: shaddah on the mim, no fathah on the hamzah): **مَا وَرَاءَ هُنَّ** → **مَّا وَرَاء هُنَّ**.

### Heading

4. p. 978 sub-heading "വിവാഹബന്ധം നിമിത്തം നിഷിദ്ധമാക്കപ്പെട്ടവർ ഇവരാണ്:": `####` → `###`. No earlier part uses `####`; printed sub-headings are `###` (e.g. Al-Baqarah part 27 "### The chief among the matters of belief are these :"), and TRANSLATING.md section 3 says `###`.

### Rendering

5. p. 978 (1), "നിഷിദ്ധം തന്നെയാണെന്നത്രെ": "are forbidden itself even if" → "are forbidden all the same even if".

### Layout

6. Six stray line breaks inside printed paragraphs, each followed by a leading space (before "For a child" p. 978, and before the markers p. 979, 980, 983, 984, 985): joined with a space, as part 04 did. No change to the text.

No dropped sentences, reversed negations, wrong categories in the lists, number errors, voice changes or added honorifics were found.
