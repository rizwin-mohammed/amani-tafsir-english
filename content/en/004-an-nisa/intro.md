## 4. Suratun-Nisa'

**سورة النساء** · Revealed in Madinah – Verses 176 – Sections (Ruku') 24 *[p. 925]*

This is a surah in which very many subjects have been dealt with, in various fields. Some subjects, such as the laws of inheritance, are a special feature of this surah. Besides there being mention of women in the very first verse, many matters concerning them have been stated in it. Therefore this surah is called ‘An-Nisa'’ (Women).

Ibn Jarir (r) quotes that Ibn Mas'ud (r), saying ‘Five ayahs in Suratun-Nisa' are dearer to me than the whole of this world’, counted the verses seen below. That is:

(1) the 31st verse, which shows that, in case one abandons the great sins, Allah will grant pardon for the other evils,

(2) the 40th verse, which shows that Allah will not do injustice even to the extent of an atom, and that He will double every good and make it great.

(3, 4) the verses 48 and 116, which state that Allah will forgive all sins other than shirk to those whom He wills,

(5) the 110th verse, which shows that, if those who do some evil, or wrong to their own selves, seek forgiveness of sins, Allah will forgive them; in the riwayah that Hakim (r) quoted, in the place of this last verse there is the 64th verse. Besides, Ibn Jarir (r) has quoted another riwayah from Ibn 'Abbas (r), in the meaning ‘Eight ayahs in Suratun-Nisa' are better than this world as far as this community is concerned’. It is, besides the five verses seen above, the verses 26, 27, 28 as well that he has specially mentioned in it. All those verses indeed are ones that signify the exceedingly great favour and the measureless mercy of Allah. They are also ones that produce in men great good hope and enthusiasm. This is the reason for these statements.
