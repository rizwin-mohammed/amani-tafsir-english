# About this translation

This is an English translation of *Vishuddha Quran Vivaranam*, the Malayalam translation and commentary (tafsir) of the Holy Quran by Muhammad Amani Moulavi. It is being translated surah by surah, and every part is checked before it is published.

## How the translation is made

1. **Translated** sentence by sentence from the Malayalam, keeping every sentence of the author. Anything uncertain is marked as a doubt instead of guessed.
2. **Checked** a second time, independently, against the pages of the book.
3. **Reviewed by a person** who reads Malayalam. Every doubt must be settled before approval.
4. **Published** only after approval. Each surah page shows its review status.

## Conventions

- **Faithful, not paraphrased.** The English follows the Malayalam sentence by sentence. The author's own bracketed additions stay in round brackets ( ).
- **Arabic kept as Arabic.** Quranic and Arabic quotations stay in Arabic script, followed by the English of the author's Malayalam rendering.
- **The Quran text** in the verse tables comes from a verified digital Uthmani text (The Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia). It is never typed by hand.
- **Verses and word meanings** are shown in tables: Arabic, the author's Malayalam, then English, so each English gloss can be checked against the Malayalam.
- **Honorifics as printed.** ﷺ after the Prophet; (r) for *radiyallahu anhu* / *rahimahullah* (the source's (റ)); (a) for *alaihissalam* (the source's (അ)).
- **Hadith abbreviations** are kept and expanded once: Mu. = Muslim, Na. = Nasa'i, Ti. = Tirmidhi.
- **Translator's notes** appear as *[TN: ...]* and are the only words not in the original.
- **Page markers** such as *[p. 145]* point to the page of the Malayalam book.

## Found a mistake?

Please use the "Report a mistake" link at the bottom of any page. Every report is read and decided by the reviewer.
