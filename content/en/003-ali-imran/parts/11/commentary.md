### Section – 11

In the previous verses, after having stated many things concerning the People of the Scripture, and addressing them, in some following verses Allah, addressing the true believers, gives many counsels and directions:-

*[Verses 102–103]*

*[p. 818]* After having alerted the true believers in the previous verses that they must guard against going astray from the way of truth, yielding to the wishes and the evil counsels of the enemies of Islam, Allah gives them some counsels that are needed so that they do not go astray thus.

(1) Allah must be guarded against according to the proper manner in which He ought to be guarded against. It is through carrying out His commands and avoiding His prohibitions that guarding against Him (taqwa) comes about. Ibn Abi Hatim (r) and others have quoted thus, as having been stated by Ibn Mas'ud (r) in the capacity of an explanation of guarding against Him according to the proper manner: ‘Being obedient to Him without showing disobedience to Him, keeping on remembering Him without forgetting Him, showing gratitude without showing ingratitude to Him: these are it.’ But, there is no need to say, surely, that to complete, settling the account according to the proper manner, all the obligations and the courtesies towards Allah is not humanly possible, and that it will not be possible for all to maintain the same standard in fulfilling them. Therefore it can be understood, on the whole, that the purport of having said to guard against Him according to the proper manner is- as was stated in Su: Taghabun, 16- that each one guard according to his own ability. That is also what many commentators of the Quran have stated. **فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ** (Guard against Allah as much as you are able) is the word that Allah has used there. Allah has declared unambiguously, surely, that Allah will not charge any individual beyond the ability of that individual ( **لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَا** ).

(2) Do not die except while being Muslims. That is, never drifting away from Islam in such a way that when dying one would not be a Muslim, one must always conduct oneself as Muslims. If a man, after having lived as a Muslim for a long time, dies as a disbeliever in the last moment, there is no benefit whatever from that Islam, surely. What is meant by being a Muslim is not, indeed, being a Muslim by caste. What is meant is indeed submitting fully to Him, obeying the injunctions and prohibitions of Allah.

*[p. 819]* (3) The rope of Allah must be held fast. The meaning of the rope or cord of Allah ( **حَبۡلِ ٱللَّهِ** ) can be seen explained by many in many words. That is: the covenant that one will obey Allah, the Quran, Islam, obeying Allah, Islamic unity, and so on. It has come in a Hadith that Ibn Jarir (r) and others have quoted: ‘The Book of Allah (the Quran) is a cord that has been let down from the heaven to the earth.’ In that respect, preference may be given to the opinion that what is meant is the Quran. Even so, if examined properly, it can be seen that the other opinions do not become contrary to this. As Imam Razi (r) has indicated, it can be said that all that is needed to arrive at the real way of success is indeed the rope of Allah. Only, some have mentioned some among them.

It is with a condition that has not been stated in the first two counsels that Allah has given this counsel. That is indeed ‘all of you ( **جَمِيعٗا** )’. What it suggests is that in holding fast the rope of Allah it is not enough for each individual to heed only his own affair, all must keep helping, cooperating with and counselling one another Making this idea clear once more, it has also been said ‘Do not become divided ( **وَلَا تَفَرَّقُواْ** )’. There is no need to tell anyone of the calamities that divisions of opinion and internal quarrels have brought about in the Muslim community up to now. Allah has warned the true believers beforehand indeed. **وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُواْ فَتَفۡشَلُواْ وَتَذۡهَبَ رِيحُكُمۡ** You (obey Allah and His Rasul. Do not quarrel with one another either. Then faint-heartedness will afflict you, and your scent (vigour) will perish. (Anfal, 46) There are many ayahs and Hadiths to quote on this subject. It is in a period in which the Muslim community keeps experiencing, more than before, the bitter result of not having heeded this warning of Allah that we are today. May Allah save us, Amin.

(4) The favour that Allah has done must be kept in remembrance. Remembering the favours that have already been received will be showing gratitude to Allah, and an incentive not to commit ingratitude, and also a cause for obtaining more favour. Although the favour of Allah is countless, in a way that does not come to an end if counted, it is a favour which, as regards the true believers of that day whom the Quran addresses first, there is no way at all to forget, and which is enough to be a permanent lesson of history for the true believers of the future, that Allah has specially mentioned here. In the time of the advent of Islam the Arab tribes, all of them, were living as enemies of one another, quarrelling with one another and shedding blood. Between the Aws tribe and the Khazraj tribe, who were residents of Madinah, especially, very fierce wars and quarrels had been continuing incessantly among them for more than a hundred years. Islam changed them all into brothers of one womb. Between the Muhajirs from Makkah and them, for its part, *[p. 820]* a companionship-life of a kind that has no match anywhere in world history came about. It is about this that it said ‘You having been enemies, Allah united between your hearts, and thus you became brothers’. Not only that, they were all also about to fall into the fire of Hell and perish, sunk in all wrong customs and evil customs up to idol worship, and in licentious acts. They had already reached up to the very brink of Hell. At whichever moment death occurs, at that very moment they would be tumbling over into the pit of Hell. This was the state. Allah, giving the favour that is Islam, rescued them from it, and turned them back to the royal road of Paradise.

After having given the said counsels, it finally alerts that the keeping on giving you such counsels through the Quran, explaining evidences and signs, is so that you may be devoted to right guidance, and that Allah explains to you without stint the evidences and signs needed for that, and that therefore everyone must guard against going astray from the straight way ( **كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ**..... ). Allah continues again:-

*[Verses 104–109]*

*[p. 822]* **مَعْرُوفِ** (ma'ruf) means ‘what is known, what is familiar’ and so on, and **مُنْكَر** (munkar) means ‘what is detested, unfamiliar’ and so on. As Imam Raghib (r) has stated, all that is known as a good thing, either through the intellect or through religion, is ‘ma'ruf’, and every thing that is detested through either of the two is ‘munkar’. The gist of what Ibn Jarir (r) has stated about these words in the explanation of the 110th verse is thus: ‘The real meaning of ma'ruf is well-familiar. Whatever thing it is good to do, and which, as regards the true believers, is not reckoned as bad, all that is ‘ma'ruf’. That obeying Allah is called ‘ma'ruf’ is because it is known and not detested with the true believers. The real meaning of ‘munkar’ is what Allah has detested and what the true believers reckon as bad. That is why it is the thing that is disobedience to Allah that is called ‘munkar’ *[p. 823]*.’ In short, all that is generally reckoned as a good thing is included in **مَعْرُوفِ**, and all that is generally reckoned as a bad thing is included in **مُنْكَر**. In Malayalam it can be given a translation according to the context as ’good custom, evil custom’, and as ‘good thing, evil thing’, and so on. What is meant by the day on which some faces become white and some others become black is the day of Qiyamah. On that day, surely, the faces of the good people will be cheerful and radiant through excess of joy, and the faces of the evil people will on that day be gloomy and darkened because of the burden of grief and excess of fear.

Allah commands that it is not enough for the true believers to look only to their personal affairs- that trying to make others good too is their duty-, and that for that purpose they need to counsel among themselves and others towards good things, and to urge towards keeping away from bad things. This command is one that applies to each individual who is a Muslim according to his ability and according to the occasion. Along with that, Allah also commands that, as in the other religious fields, there needs always to be in this community a body that keeps on going down into this field too and working. The Prophet ﷺ has graciously said thus: ‘If anyone among you sees an evil custom (a detested thing), let him change it with his hand. Then, if that is not possible for him, with his tongue (let him change it by speaking). Then, if he has no ability for that either, with his heart (let him detest it). That, for its part, is also the weakest in true faith.’ (Mu.) In one riwayah there is this too: ‘Beyond that there is not, moreover, a mustard seed's measure of true faith.’ How far the seriousness is that Islam has assigned to this subject can be understood from this sacred saying. There are, moreover, plenty of Quran sentences and sayings of the Prophet to quote concerning this. By way of example, remember here only some Hadiths:-

(1). The gist of a Hadith that Ibn Mas'ud (r) has quoted as having been said by the Prophet ﷺ: ‘When the Children of Isra'il were sunk in sin, the scholars who were among them prohibited them. They did not desist. Then they took to cooperating with them in sitting together (in companionship) and in food and drink. Then Allah made their hearts strike against one another. (made them split apart) He also cursed them through the tongues of the prophets Dawud, Sulaiman *[DOUBT: "സുലൈമാൻ" is clearly printed; the hadith as it is usually quoted (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi), like Ma'idah 78, names only Dawud and 'Isa son of Maryam. Either the author's wording or a slip; the reviewer may want to check.]* and 'Isa (a)...’ (Da ; Ti.)

(2). In a Hadith that Abu Sa'id al-Khudri (r) quotes, the Prophet ﷺ says: ‘Among the greatest jihad (righteous struggle) is saying a word of justice in the presence of a wrongdoing Sultan (ruler)’ (Da; Ti.)

(3) A saying of the Prophet that Hudhaifah ibn al-Yaman (r) quoted: ‘By Him in whose *[p. 824]* hand my soul is, by Him indeed! You must indeed command good custom, and forbid concerning evil custom. Otherwise, it may come about that Allah sends upon you some punitive action from His presence. Afterwards, when you call on Him and pray, He will not give you an answer.’ (A; Ti; Ja.) I am not lengthening by quoting more Hadiths. Allah also makes known to us, Allah *[TN: "അല്ലാഹു" (Allah) is printed twice here: "അല്ലാഹു നമ്മെ അല്ലാഹു അറിയിക്കുന്നു"; the second appears to be a printing slip. Translated as printed.]*, that it is only for the community that counsels with good things and forbids concerning evil things that there will be success and progress.( **وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ** )

The People of the Scripture are those in whom, after they had received sufficient proofs through the prophets and the scriptures, and had accepted true faith, having understood the truth properly, confusions of ideas and differences of opinion about that truth gradually arose, and partisanship and splitting apart came about among one another. Allah also counsels the true believers that you too must not become like them. They did not hold fast to the rope of Allah. They did not conduct themselves in agreement and affection with one another. Nor did they give good counsel to one another and restrain from evil ways. Had they done so, they would not have arrived at a deplorable state of this kind. In the Hereafter, for its part, what is for them is only a very severe punishment and disgrace. Therefore, so that experiences like theirs may not come about among you, and so that you may clearly understand the way in which you ought to walk, We have already given you all the proofs and the explanation that are needed. Allah has no intention at all of showing any injustice to anyone in the world. That is why all this much is being given, repeating and explaining. All things in the universe are indeed the creation and the slaves of Allah. It is also to Him indeed that all comes to its final end. Then all His creatures are obliged, surely, to walk only through the way that He directs, and not to adopt the ways that become a cause of His displeasure and detestation. Such, and so on, is the summary of the idea contained in verses 105–109. **الله اعلم**

As long as it went on remembering that mighty warning of Allah as was due, the Muslim community was indeed in success and in goodness. From whatever time a slackening came in the mind of the community regarding the seriousness of that warning, from that time onwards - according to the measure of that slackening- its consequence too kept on appearing among them. Today, for its part, it has reached its farthest end, and the prophecy that the Rasul of Allah made has come true among them letter for letter. May Allah save the community. Amin, Mu'awiyah (r) made known to the people thus, that the noble Prophet ﷺ had graciously said: ‘The People of the Scripture split up into seventy-two parties in their religion. This community may in due course split up into seventy-three people of ways- into self-interested parties-. Except *[p. 825]* one, all the rest will be in Hell. That indeed is ‘the Jama'ah’. (the real Muslim band) In my community some people will in due course come out. Just as rabies spreads and pervades in a man whom rabies has seized, self-wills will spread and pervade in them. Not a single vein or joint of his will remain without its entering, surely.’ This Hadith, which Imam Ahmad (r) has narrated, is indeed a famous Hadith that has come through many routes too.
