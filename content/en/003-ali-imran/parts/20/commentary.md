### Section – 19

*[Verses 181–182]*

*[p. 904]* When, encouraging the spending of wealth in the path of Allah, **مَّن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يُقۡرِضُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا** (who is it that gives Allah a good loan?! The gist is that for all this He will not fail to give them punishment, settling the account. To many realities in this manner the last sentence, **أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَيۡسَ بِظَلَّامٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ** (Allah is not at all one who does injustice to the slaves), points the finger.

*[Verses 183–184]*

*[p. 906]* It reproaches, pointing out a false claim that the Jews fabricated. That we must not believe any messenger of God unless he performs a rite of sacrifice and shows it, divine fire coming and consuming it, is what Allah has commanded us. Therefore, their babble was that, without performing such a rite of sacrifice, there is no way for us to believe Muhammad. Very well, if that is so, many Rasuls who passed away among you before, both with many clear signs, and showing the very sacrifice-sign that you babble about, you have made out to be false, and put to death, surely. If your claim is right, what is the reason for doing so?! Your history itself is witness, surely, to this claim being a sheer lie. thus Allah instructs the Prophet ﷺ to give reply to them. If, after it has become clear that all such claims of theirs are only a swindle, they are still not ready to accept the truth, there is no need to feel aggrieved or to grieve at that. Many Rasuls who brought sufficient proofs, and divine records, and the scripture that clearly explains truth and untruth, have been rejected before this. He also consoles the Prophet ﷺ that this rejection is not anything happening for the first time.

Many kinds of rites of sacrifice can be seen in the Bible under names such as offering, burnt offering, atonement. (Look at Judges: 6, at 21; 13, at 20; Leviticus: 9, at 24 and so on.) Animals such as the bull, their portions, eatables such as grain, all these used to be used for that. Mostly, the custom was that all of them were burnt, being set on fire on the altars of offering according to fixed rules. As some Quran commentators and the Bible point out, among them there were also some kinds of rites of sacrifice in which an invisible divine fire came and destroyed the things offered. (\*) Although many of such rites of offering are usually counted as ‘mu'jizat’ (superhuman event)s that manifest at the hands of prophets, the Bible does not state it as a means for establishing the prophethood, or the divine mission, of a prophet. Then, it is proved from the Bible itself that the said claim of the Jews is a lie. Even so, as Allah has stated, for that sign, the rite of offering performed by a prophet being consumed by divine fire, *[p. 907]* being seen and still that prophet being rejected and attempts being made to kill him, an example can be seen in the Bible itself. Allah's statement does not need the testimony of the Bible. Even so, it will serve, surely, to understand that their own scripture in the possession of the People of the Book itself bears testimony that the claim of the Jews is hollow. Its summary is this :-

*[Verses 185–186]*

*[p. 909]* These are two verses containing good news for the good people, and a huge warning for the evil people. All are ones who taste death. No one has exemption from it. It will be only on the Day of Qiyamah that the fruits of the deeds of all are given, settling the account. What the good people will receive will be Paradise, and the evil people Hell. There is no third place to be received. If one gets freed from Hell and receives entry into Paradise, there is no other good fortune greater than that. If what one receives is the opposite, there is also no misfortune greater than that. Then, to whatever hardships and losses and trials the good people may have been subjected while in this world, all that will only make easy the way to that great good fortune. Directly opposite, however many comforts and conveniences the evil people may have received while here, those too will be ones that light up the path to Hell. Or, the joys and sorrows in this life are not the criteria of real good fortune and misfortune. The worldly life, if looked at altogether, is a collection of artificial resources. Nothing is permanent. Much that is made to seem good will in reality be harmful. The opposite also will be there. Therefore no one must be deceived by it. That, because the true believers are ones who become objects of Allah's liking, they will not have to experience any difficulties in this life, must not be supposed. In their own selves and properties and all, they may indeed be subjected to many a trial. Without distinction as People of the Book or otherwise, they will also have to face many kinds of harm and disturbance from all kinds of disbelievers. What they must do is to adopt patience in all that, and to keep observing caution without violating the rules and directions of Allah. That, for its part, is not merely a trivial matter. It is serious and indispensable. Such are the ideas these verses contain.

Lack of rain, flood, destruction of crops, shortage of production, the need for expenditure exceeding income, trade loss and the like, all these are trials faced in properties. War, the attack of enemies, disease, captivity, death, wound, fear, grief and such are trials in selves, indeed. Having to experience persecutions from the People of the *[p. 910]* Book and from the mushriks can be of many kinds. All the reproaches, mockeries, threats, counter-propaganda and all that affect Islam, or the Prophet ﷺ, or the Muslims, or the Quran, or religious symbols, or moral values, or the laws of the Shari'ah, will be included in it. As Ibn Kathir (r) has pointed out, it is natural that those who engage in fields such as religious preaching, propagation of the truth, the struggle against superstitions, have to face many adverse circumstances, and opposing forces. Keeping patience and caution, relying on Allah, is indeed the chief remedy that exists for that too. **والله الموفق**
