*[Verses 191–194]*

*[p. 915]* The infinitely vast sky situated in the upper region, the countless great star-spheres, planets, satellites, their regulated and differing movements, the extremely distant paths of travel, and so many things. many things; this earth, the oceans in it, continents islands, mountains, deserts, forests, lands bird, beast and such living beings, products minerals *[DOUBT: printed ഘനനങ്ങൾ (300 dpi crop), not a known word; probably a slip for ഖനനങ്ങൾ / ഖനിജങ്ങൾ ("mined things, minerals"). Options: "minerals" / "solid masses" (from ഘനം, "solid").]*, and so many, many things night and day changing, one after the other, and, when one decreases, the other going on increasing. For those who think about the relation between the course of the sun's motion and it (\*) and the like, about the most excellent great attributes of Allah, the Creator and the Controller of all, such as oneness, omniscience, omnipotence, skill in creation, *[p. 916]* it becomes possible to understand more. How these creatures, including man, came into being, from where they came, to where they go, what the aim of man's life must be, what his place here is, what his future will be: to such subjects it will also give him real light.

> **Note:** (\*) About this it is explained in the commentary on Su: Anbiya': 33.

But, only for those who have the remembering and the awareness of Allah on all occasions, without the distinction of when standing, when sitting, and when lying, do they become real signs. As far as those who do not have the remembrance of Allah and the awareness of Him are concerned, all of them can be seen only as mere pranks of nature. As the fruit of their thinking, they may be able to bring out some marvellous news that startles the world, or, making interplanetary journeys, to carry out some handling on the upper spheres. But, about the realities pointed out above they are not going to get any reach or grip at all. The response that those signs produce in the minds, and on the tongues, of the men of good reason who reflect intently through spheres of thought grounded in the remembrance of Allah is those prayer-hymns that call out ‘Our Rabb, our Rabb. Yes their faith in Allah, and in the prophets, and in their preaching, grows, becoming stronger on account of their thinking. It also increases for them the eagerness for the mercy and the favour from Allah's presence. It also produces more dread about His wrath and punishment. They will also keep praying to Allah that they may, living as good men and dying as good men, bring (things) to a close with the life of the Hereafter made comfortable.

By what was said as ‘the one who invites to true faith’ the Rasul is meant. What is meant by the matter promised through the Rasuls. is the rewards and favours that they made known, through the scriptures or through counsels, that the righteous will receive in the Hereafter. The gist of a hadith that many, such as Bukhari, Muslim (r), have quoted as said by Ibn 'Abbas (r) is thus: ‘I stayed one night in the house of Maimunah (r), my maternal aunt and the wife of the Prophet ﷺ. The noble one and his household lay along the length of the pillow, and I across its breadth. When it was midnight the noble one rose. To remove the drowsiness of sleep he stroked his face with his hand. Then the noble one recited these verses beginning with **إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ**.... to the end ( 190- 194). Then the noble one took wudu and stood to pray. I too, doing likewise, went and stood near. He took hold of my right ear and stood me at his right side. Then he prayed ten rak'ahs, two at a time, and then one single rak'ah (witr). By then the call to prayer for Subh was given. With that, praying two light rak'ahs (the sunnah of Subh), he went to the mosque and performed the Subh prayer; Ibn 'Abbas (r) was a child at that time. It has also come in some riwayahs that his father 'Abbas (r) had sent him so as to learn and know the noble one's night prayer and the like.

*[p. 917]* For the thinkers who are men of good reason and true believers, when they think about the creation of the heavens and earth, the result that becomes manifest in them on account of the signs they are able to discover is, surely, their prayers seen above. How Allah evaluates those prayers, see in the next verse. Allah says:-

*[Verse 195]*

*[p. 918]* Yes, without delay Allah will accept their prayer. ‘If My servants ask you about Me (do say): I am one who is near. I will give answer to the prayer of the one who prayed.’ (2: 186) Allah has promised thus, surely. For the good deeds each person has done I will give the very best reward. Nothing will I waste in vain. There is no distinction of male and female in it. Mankind, surely, is made up of male and female together the distinction of woman and man has no place in receiving the fruit of deeds. But, those who have to endure sacrifices such as having to go away abandoning homeland and the like in the path of Allah- that is, in the matter of His din-, being expelled from their own land and from home, having to experience wrong and oppression, waging war, being killed, will indeed receive special ranks. Their sins and faults and offences I will let go and forgive them. I will also honour them, giving them the most delightful life of Paradise. Such is the import of the answer given to them. It is only with Allah, surely, that the best and highest rewards are ready. ( **وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥ حُسۡنُ ٱلثَّوَابِ** )

*[Verses 196–198]*

*[p. 919]* Seeing the disbelievers who are deniers of the truth roaming at their ease in this world as they like, and leading a comfortable life, no one needs to be deceived. None of the comforts and conveniences in the worldly life is a sign or a criterion of Allah's pleasure and love. All that is merely a paltry and temporary enjoyment only. What is going to come to them afterwards will be the punishment of Hell, wretched to the utmost extent. Directly opposite, for the people who observe caution, obeying the injunctions and prohibitions of Allah- whatever their condition in this life may have been- it will be Paradise, full of comfort and blessed. That indeed is the hospitality they will receive when they go into the presence of Allah. That, for its part, is also everlasting. There is no other reward better than the reward Allah has made ready for the people who are holy ones *[DOUBT: പുണ്യവാളൻമാർ (clear on the image) usually means "saints, holy ones"; here it stands for the verse's ٱلۡأَبۡرَار, which the panel renders പുണ്യവാൻമാർ "meritorious ones". Options: "holy ones" / "saintly ones" / "meritorious ones".]*.

*[Verse 199]*

*[p. 920]* Concerning the People of the Scripture, and the hypocrites (munafiqs), many mentions and reproaches have passed in this surah before this. Allah has not stated or indicated as excepted from those reproaches even some individuals or other among the hypocrites. Because hypocrites are always hypocrites indeed. But, as far as the People of the Scripture are concerned, although they are in general subject to all the reproaches of Allah- at the same time -a few good individuals too are, in reality, not lacking among them it is about them that this verse points out. They are not obstinate ones who would believe only in their own scripture. They are ones who believe in Allah, and in the Quran, and in the Prophet ﷺ, and who have God-fearing reverence towards Allah. As others do, they do not cover up, or alter and overturn, or misinterpret the truth for worldly practical gains. That such individuals will have a special reward with Allah, and that they are meritorious ones. and that for Allah, quickly settling their reckoning, *[p. 921]* to give the reward they deserve is not at all difficult: it announces (these) to them as good news.

Since it has been said that they believe in what was sent down to you as well- that is, in the Quran as well-, it is clear that what is meant by this is individuals from the People of the Scripture who accepted Islam and believed in the Prophet ﷺ. The hollowness of what some Christian priests are wont to rattle off, quoting some Quran sentences like this leaving out what is before and after them, that it is stated in the Quran that there is salvation also for Christians who do not believe in the noble Prophet ﷺ, can be understood from this. The gist of a saying of the Prophet that Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (r) quoted: ‘To three parties Allah will give their wage twofold. That is, the one from the People of the Scripture who believed in his own prophet, and in me; the slave who fulfilled the duty to Allah and the duty to his master; and the one who, after training his own slave-woman well in knowledge and manners, set her free, and afterwards married her.’ (Bu; Mu.)

*[Verse 200]*

After explaining many subjects that the true believers must know, and learn, and practise, as a jewel-summary of them, as it were, Allah concludes this surah giving four counsels that contain merely four words only. Relating to one's own selves, relating to others, relating to Allah: thus there are three kinds of subjects that men have to face. The whole gist of the stance that man must adopt in all three of these spheres is contained in these counsels. Putting it through another side, for success, both worldly and of the Hereafter, *[p. 922]* this is a verse that contains the essence of the ways. With the address **يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ** (O you who have believed), Allah says:-

(1) **ٱصۡبِرُواْ** (you be patient): the import is that in observing the laws and counsels of Allah, in fulfilling duties, in working good deeds, in facing calamities and hardships, in all of these indeed, patience and endurance must be adopted. This counsel is one that affects all personal spheres.

(2) **وَصَابِرُواْ** (you show excellence in patience). The import is that in all kinds of harms and hardships that may be faced from all sections such as families, neighbours, one's own people, opposing parties, allied parties, enemies made up of men and devils, you must show zeal to stand excelling them in patience. In wars, and in encounters with enemies, displaying steadfastness of mind and courage such as to frighten the opposing party is the aspect that deserves the most importance in this sphere.

(3) **وَرَابِطُواْ** (and you keep on keeping fast guard). That is, the gist is that you must keep being vigilant about attacks that may arise from outside- especially from the side of enemies-, and keep always getting ready to meet them. This verb form is from the root **رَبَاطُ** (rabat). This word is mostly used in a technical sense: keeping fast guard with cavalry in the border regions, for the protection of the border and for observing and learning of the attacks of the enemies. It can be understood from the hadiths seen below that that word is used also in the sense of keeping away from outside matters, concentrating attention on one important matter. Although in a way this counsel can be included in the second counsel, it has been specially singled out and mentioned in view of its importance. The second and the third counsels are indeed to be practised in all harms that arise from outside.

The noble Prophet ﷺ says:

1\. One day's ‘ribat’ (fast guard-keeping) in the path of Allah is better than this world and what is situated in it.’ (Bu)

2\. Doing ‘ribat’ for a night and a day is better than a month's fasting and (night) prayer. If he dies in it, the deed he used to do (its fruit) will also go on continuing to come to him.... (Mu) What is intended in these sayings of the Prophet is ‘ribat’ in the technical sense explained above. **اللّه اعلم**

*[p. 923]* 3. Shall I make known to you some things by which sins are forgiven and ranks are raised? Doing ‘wudu’ well even in unpleasant conditions (such as cold), increasing the footsteps (going and coming) to the mosques. Waiting for another prayer after one prayer, this indeed is ‘ribat’ (Mu; Ti) What is mentioned in this hadith is ribat in the general sense.

4\. **وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ** (and you guard against Allah) What is meant by guarding against Allah is, surely, having God-fearing reverence towards Him by way of obeying the injunctions and prohibitions of Allah. Then all matters relating to Allah are contained in this word. For those who accept these four counsels in the proper manner, success- everywhere and always- is quite certain indeed. That is what Allah points out as the last word. **لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ** (you may attain success). Yes, the gist is that it is for that that these counsels are given to you. **والله الموفق والمعين**
