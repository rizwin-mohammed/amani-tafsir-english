*[Verses 69–71]*

*[p. 787]* What they are doing is that, while they keep understanding and confirming the evidences that are in their scriptures and in their knowledge as proof of the truthfulness of the noble Prophet ﷺ, along with that they cover them up, and, mixing together truth and untruth, do not make the reality clear. Allah reproaches them, asking for what purpose it is that you keep doing such wayward acts.

### Section – 8

Allah exposes an intrigue that the People of the Scripture carry out, intending to lead the true believers astray because of envy and rancour towards the Prophet ﷺ and towards the true believers:-

*[Verses 72–74]*

*[p. 788]* The People of the Scripture- the Jews especially- are lying in wait for a chance to see the true believers go astray. It is a programme that they plotted in secret for that purpose that Allah discloses in the 72nd verse. To put up some from among themselves; they are to go in the morning and, saying that they have accepted true faith, put on the guise of Muslims; after mixing with the Muslims for some time, when it becomes evening, they are also to make themselves outcasts from Islam *[p. 789]*. ‘Thinking that Islam is the true religion we entered it, is it not only now that we have understood its inside story. Therefore, here we have come back from it.’ They are also to spread such things about. When they see this, at least some true believers who are of little intelligence and who do not know the story will be caused to leave Islam, thinking that, since these people who have acquaintance with the scripture are saying so, this will be the very truth, and, exploiting them, they can create confusion of ideas among the people against Islam, such things are their calculation They would also specially advise these puppets *[DOUBT: the word is കോമരങ്ങൾ, literally the possessed oracle-dancers of temple rites; here it is used of the men put up for the plot. "Puppets" or "mouthpieces" may be meant.]* that, whatever anyone says or does against the religious practices that you have been believing and observing, you must not- even if you agree with the tongue- believe any of it with the mind.

Not only does He expose this secret stratagem of theirs, He also commands the Prophet ﷺ to make them understand that they are not going to get any benefit at all from it, and that it is only a display of envy and of foolishness. It is Allah who shows right guidance, and who gives the good fortune to attain it. He has not laid down any rule that He will give favour only to such-and-such groups or community. Allah is not at all one crooked of mind like you. He is very Vast and One who knows everything. He will favour those whom He intends. To those whom He intends He will specially pour out mercy. He knows to whom all favour and mercy ought to be given. One reason for this is your envy at another people (the Muslims) having come to receive a scripture and a prophet, as you received. Another reason is your vain desire that they must not become ones who have attained right guidance, in such a way as to give occasion for the true believers, when they go before Allah, to defeat you by arguing that you are ones who rejected the truth knowingly indeed. It is this that impelled you to such base deeds. Know that you will not succeed in any of this. Such things are the purport of what Allah went on to say.

The sentences **أَن يُؤۡتَىٰٓ أَحَدٞ ………… عِندَ رَبِّكُمۡ** in the 73rd verse (beginning ‘as you have been given’ and up to ‘because they will argue’), it is as a part of the reply that the Prophet ﷺ ought to give them that we described above, and adopted in the translation there is also a possibility that this is the continuation of the statement of the People of the Scripture, and that the sentence **قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ** (Say that the guidance of Allah is guidance) was commanded to the Prophet ﷺ in between. It is also in this way that many of the commentators are seen to have adopted it. Then, the purport of those sentences can be understood thus: If these- the true believers- stand firm in their religion, it will come about that, besides us, those who are like these *[p. 790]* too have received favours such as the scripture, as we received. It will also give occasion for them to present a case against us in the presence of Allah. Therefore we ought not to give them the opportunity for that. Either way, the intended meaning is clear. Some other opinions too can be seen stated here by the Quran commentators.

*[Verses 75–76]*

*[p. 791]* About the meanings of **قِنطَارٖ** it has already been described in the explanation of the 14th verse. Heap- very much wealth- is what is meant. People who have no knowledge of letters- who do not know writing and reading - is the meaning of the word **أُمِّيِّـۧنَ**. It is the Arabs, who had no acquaintance with the scripture, that are meant.

Many evil deeds of the People of the Scripture were told before, surely. But, all the individuals among them are not alike. There are among them also people of good disposition who, even if a very great amount of wealth is entrusted to them, give it back at the proper time. Quite contrary to that, the excuse that those who are not ready to give back even a single gold coin if it is given to them in trust- except that it may perhaps be got back in case one keeps pressing that it must be given back- give for it is this: there is no obligation at all that the liabilities towards those who are not People of the Scripture must be fulfilled, using their wealth is not culpable at all, it is only keeping the duties towards those belonging to one's own community that is a duty for us, such things. This is only a false claim that they themselves fabricate in the name of Allah. Pact settlements and trustworthiness must be kept with whomever it may be. It is those who keep pacts, and keep guarding the commands and prohibitions of Allah, whom Allah will love. Others will not receive His love. Such things are the gist of these verses.

There is no need to show leniency in dealings with those who are not of one's own race. There is no objection either to taking interest from them. With those of one's own race one must not press hard in demanding a debt, or take interest- such things the Jews have traditionally kept believing. (Deuteronomy 15: 3, 23: 20) Some such blind notions are wont to creep in among some ignorant lay Muslims too. In reality, in keeping justice and trustworthiness there is no discrimination as Muslim or non-Muslim. See a hadith that Ibn Abi Hatim (r) quotes. Its gist is as follows: When the People of the Scripture said, ‘In the matter of the unlettered there is no way for any liability to come about upon us ( **لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡنَا فِي ٱلۡأُمِّيِّـۧنَ سَبِيلٞ** )’, the Prophet ﷺ said: ‘The enemies of Allah are telling a lie. There is not any matter (of this kind) that was in the Jahiliyyah that has not been put under these two feet of mine. (All have been discarded) Except the amanah (the matter *[p. 792]* of trustworthiness). As for that, it ought to be discharged to the virtuous one and to the wicked one.’

*[Verse 77]*

Remember what was stated in the explanation of Al-Baqarah : the 27th verse about the meaning and intent of **عَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ** (Allah's- the one with Allah- pact). To believe in the Prophet ﷺ, to follow the way that he brought, the resolve and vow that one will do such things, is what is mainly meant here Allah warns that for those who, without fulfilling the pacts and solemn oaths that they have taken on as a liability to Allah, are satisfied, in exchange for them, with a trifling price- worldly material gains-, there is no good or salvation whatsoever to be received in the Hereafter, and that not even a particle of mercy, or of love, or of pardon will they receive from Allah, and that along with that they will have to suffer a very severe punishment too. That it was said that Allah will not speak to them, and will not look toward them, indicates the gravity of Allah's indignation towards them.

Concerning many matters too that are connected with the warning in this noble verse, and that are included in its scope, and that we must keep in mind in daily life, it has come *[p. 793]* in the hadiths of the Prophet ﷺ. As an example, one or two may be quoted:

(1) Abu Dharr (r) says: The Rasul ﷺ said: ‘There are three parties: on the Day of Qiyamah Allah will not speak to them, nor will He look toward them, nor will He refine them.’ I said; ‘They have lost out and been disgraced, surely! Who are they, O Rasul?’ The noble one, having repeated that word three times, said: ‘He who walks trailing his garment on the ground, and he who sells off merchandise by means of a false oath, and he who brings up the benefaction he has done.’ (Ah; Mu.)

(2) In a hadith narrated from many Companions, the Prophet ﷺ says: ‘If anyone swears a false oath in order to seize a person's property, he will meet Allah as One who has become angry with him.’ (Ah; Bu: Mu)

*[Verse 78]*

*[p. 794]* It is about a grave fraud that the Jewish scholars are wont to commit in the scripture that it states. When reading out the scripture, to read it altering letters and words, or putting them out of place, or making additions and reductions in it, so that a meaning in keeping with their own interests comes about. People without knowledge will suppose, surely, that all that is scripture sentences. Their aim too is that very thing. It will also be in the manner that all that they read is the statements of the scripture, and what Allah has sent down, that they do the reading. There is no need to say, surely, that this is not a blunder that occurs in interpretation or in opinion, and that it is the fabricating of a sheer lie in the name of Allah.

Some forgeries more or less like this are sometimes seen among some Muslim scholars too who are people of crooked interests. They do not alter the letters or the words of the Quran. Nor is that at all possible. Even to those who do not know the meaning of the Quran, the words in it are, surely, things known with full certainty. Therefore what they will do is to give an interpretation of meaning to the words, or to the sentences, in it in a way that agrees with their own opinions, and then to establish all that as statements of the Quran. Such people too ought to fear the severe warning that has gone before in Su: Al-Baqarah 79, in the course of telling about some corrupt practices of the People of the Scripture, concerning those who write the book with their own hands, and then, for the sake of a trifling material gain, state that it is from Allah.

*[Verses 79–80]*

*[p. 795]* **عِبَاد** ('Ibad) is the plural of the word **عَبْد** ('Abd). It is used both in the meaning ‘slave’ who is owned *[DOUBT: the print has ഉടമയായ, and ഉടമ usually means "owner"; here it must describe the slave as a possession ("who is owned property"). Whether that is the intended sense, or a slip (e.g. for ഉടമയുള്ള, "who has an owner"), is not certain.]*, and in the meaning ‘servant’ who is a worshipper. When it is in the first meaning- as Imam Raghib (r) has pointed out the plural comes as **عَبِيد** ('Abid), and when it is in the second meaning as **عِبَاد** ('Ibad). **رَبَانِى** (Rabbaniyy): its plural is **رَبَانِيُون** (Rabbaniyyuna). It can be seen given a signification in such a way that meanings such as ‘one learned in the scripture, religious leader, religious preceptor, ruling leader, holy man’ can be stated for it. It is on the basis of the fact that meanings such as ‘one who rears, sustainer, lord, one who gives training, master’ come in the language for the word **رَب** (Rabb), which is the root word in this, and of the fact that it is wont to be used as an attribute-name of Allah, that all the meanings mentioned above have been ascribed to it. What has been stated here as the reason for saying that they must be ‘Rabbani’s is ‘you have been teaching the scripture and *[p. 796]* studying’ ( **بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تُعَلِّمُونَ** ), surely. Therefore it can be taken that what is meant here is the religious preceptors and those learned in religion who keep knowing and counselling the scriptural knowledge. There are opinions that this word is an actual Arabic word, and, no, that it is an 'Abrani (Hebrew) word, and so on. In any case, some words in a form that has similarity to this in sound and in meaning can be seen used in many places in the Bible. Therefore there is no doubt that it is a word that was well familiar among the People of the Scripture from before. (\*)

Since the word ‘Rabb’ has the meaning great one, it will be used for great officials.... Later this came to be used specially for the scribes who were experts in the science of the scripture,’ Under the heading ‘Rabbani’ it says again: ‘Those who speak the Aramaic (Arami) language say ‘Rabbuna for the word ‘Rabbi’ in the Hebrew language. The gist of the idea can be understood in brief thus: A human being who has received the scripture and wisdom- judgeship according to the laws of the scripture- and prophethood, must never say to the people that you must be servants who worship me. Nor will that happen. On the contrary, they will only say that you must become ones learned in the scripture who keep studying and knowing the scripture, and counselling and teaching others, and worshipping and obeying Allah. In the same way, no one such as that will command either to take, other than Allah alone- angels or prophets- as Rabbs. The prophets are ones who have been commissioned for the establishment and propagation of tawhid. After you have become Muslims who have submitted to Allah according to their preaching, how will they then counsel kufr and shirk (disbelief and polytheism), which are diametrically opposed to it?! If one sees your state of affairs, it is like that that it would seem, surely. In reality none of that is going to happen.

These verses are ones reproaching specially the Christians, who claim to be the followers of the Prophet 'Isa (a), deeming him Rabb and keeping on worshipping him, and generally all those who, claiming to be followers of the prophets, ascribe divinity to others than Allah and perform acts of worship to them. The prophets and the angels are, surely, the intermediaries and messengers between Allah and mankind. If even to them divine status must not be ascribed and acts of worship must not be offered, there is no need to say specially that to anyone at all such as the scholars *[p. 797]*, priests, shaikhs, great men who are followers of the prophets- however high in rank they may be- the rank of divinity must not be given to make them Rabbs. The purport of making Rabbs is not only openly offering acts of worship to them, or conceding to them too anything belonging to the special attributes of Allah's rights of authority. It has already become clear from the 64th verse that to ascribe a place in religion to the commands and prohibitions of others as though they were the commands and prohibitions of Allah is also making them Rabb.
