## Corrections made (before → after)

### Words dropped or added

1. p. 827, "‘നിങ്ങളാണ് ഏറ്റവും ഉത്തമ സമുദായം’": ഏറ്റവും was not rendered (the verse's ഏറ്റം ഉത്തമ is "the very best"). "Having said ‘It is you who are the best community’" → "Having said ‘It is you who are the very best community’".
2. v. 116, "അല്ലാഹുവിൽ നിന്നു(ള്ള ശിക്ഷ) യാതൊന്നും": the bracket only completes നിന്ന് into നിന്നുള്ള ശിക്ഷ; the English said "from Allah" twice. "anything at all from Allah (the punishment from Allah)" → "anything at all (of the punishment) from Allah".
3. Footnote p. 829, "കഴിഞ്ഞ രണ്ടാം ലോക മഹായുദ്ധം": no comma is printed and കഴിഞ്ഞ qualifies the war. "After the last, Second World War ended" → "After the last Second World War ended".

### Renderings not as settled in earlier parts

4. തോന്നിയവാസികൾ (v. 110, its table, and the quotation of it on p. 834): Al-Baqarah 2:26 and 2:99 and part 09 (v. 82 table, its renderings item and open doubt 6) render the person-noun "the wayward", keeping "licentiousness / licentious acts" for തോന്നിയവാസം (parts 08–10). v. 110 "most of them are indeed licentious ones." → "most of them are indeed wayward ones."; table "are indeed licentious (defiant- rogue) ones" → "are indeed wayward (defiant- rogue) ones"; p. 834 "‘Most of them are licentious ones’" → "‘Most of them are wayward ones’".
5. v. 113, "‘സജൂദ്’ [സാഷ്ടാംഗനമസ്കാരം]": part 04 (3:43, "‘സുജൂദ്’ [സാഷ്ടാംഗ നമസ്കാരം]") renders the bracket "[prayer in full prostration]". "‘sajud’ [prostration prayer]" → "‘sajud’ [prayer in full prostration]".
