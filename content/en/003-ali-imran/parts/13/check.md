## Corrections made (before → after)

### Arabic not as printed

1. p. 838, عض الانامل: the PDF places the one fatha of this word at x = 359.52, inside the box of the ن glyph (358.67–361.83) and outside the box of the lam-alif and alif before it (361.67–370.20); on the same page the marks of ظَهَارَة sit at their own base letters (ظ 318.60, mark 319.56; ه 313.56, mark 313.56). The 600 dpi crop shows the fatha over the tooth of the ن, to the left of the lam-alif. The translator's text had the fatha on the alif. `**عض الاَنامل**` → `**عض الانَامل**`.

### Renderings not as settled in earlier parts

2. v. 118 word table, "ഈർഷ്യത, വൈരാഗ്യം": വൈരാഗ്യം is "rancour" in Al-Baqarah parts 25 and 33 (new-terms tables). "resentment, animosity" → "resentment, rancour".
3. p. 840, "… കീഴിൽ ജീവിക്കുന്ന (**) വിമതസ്ഥരെപ്പോലും": വിമതസ്ഥർ is "being of a different religion" in Al-Baqarah part 28 (its first occurrence). "even those of other faiths who live under them" → "even those of a different religion who live under them".
4. വെറുപ്പ്, two English words in one part: v. 120 "അതവർക്ക് വെറുപ്പ് (അഥവാ വ്യസനം) ഉണ്ടാക്കുന്നു" had "displeasure" and p. 838 "അവരോടുള്ള വെറുപ്പും പ്രതിഷേധവും" had "dislike" (which Al-Baqarah part 34 keeps for അനിഷ്ടം). Al-Baqarah part 34 renders വെറുപ്പ് "aversion". v. 120 "it causes displeasure (or grief) to them" → "it causes aversion (or grief) to them"; p. 838 "the registering of dislike and protest towards them" → "the registering of aversion and protest towards them". The table's verb "അതവരെ വെറുപ്പിക്കും (വ്യസനിപ്പിക്കും)" "it will displease them (grieve them)" is kept (see open doubt 6).

### Translator's notes

5. p. 845, "(വാവി ‘ന്’ ‘ഇ’ കാരത്തോടും": the TN on the misspelt ending of വാവിന് and the unclosed bracket is removed. Under the practice of parts 08–12 (part 11 item 2) TNs are kept only for evident slips such as a misspelt name, a wrong reference or a repeated word; missing stops, quote marks and brackets are mirrored without TN, and a misspelt common word is translated by its meaning (part 11: കുണ്ടിന്റ). The English already reads "to the waw" and mirrors the unclosed bracket. "… to the waw *[TN: printed "വാവി ‘ന്’", …]* (as ‘musawwimin’ …" → "… to the waw (as ‘musawwimin’ …".

Escaped as `(\*)` and `(\*\*)` in the paragraph and in both notes, as parts 10 and the Al-Baqarah parts write them. The text is unchanged.
