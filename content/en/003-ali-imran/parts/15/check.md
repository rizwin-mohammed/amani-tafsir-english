## Corrections made (before → after)

### Tense not as printed

1. v. 140, "അല്ലാഹുവാകട്ടെ, അക്രമികളെ ഇഷ്ടപ്പെടുകയില്ല": the -ഉകയില്ല form is "will not", as in this verse's own table ("സ്നേഹിക്കുക (ഇഷ്ടപ്പെടുക)യില്ല" "will not love (like)"), 3:57 in part 06 ("അല്ലാഹു അക്രമികളെ സ്നേഹിക്കുകയില്ല" "Allah will not love the wrongdoers") and Al-Baqarah part 31 ("ഇഷ്ടപ്പെടുകയില്ല" "will not like"). "Allah, for His part, does not like the wrongdoers." → "Allah, for His part, will not like the wrongdoers."

### Modal not as printed

2. p. 862, hadith, "അല്ലാഹുവിനോട് (അതിൽ നിന്ന്) ഒഴിവാക്കിത്തരുവാൻ ചോദിച്ചുകൊള്ളണം": the -ണം ending is "must", as the sentences before and after it in the same hadith ("മോഹിക്കരുത്" "must not yearn", "ക്ഷമ കൈക്കൊള്ളണം" "must hold to patience") and p. 867 "സ്വീകരിച്ചുകൊള്ളണം" "must adopt". "You should ask Allah to spare you (from it)." → "You must ask Allah to spare you (from it)."

### Meaning sharpened

3. p. 862, "സ്വർഗത്തിൽ പ്രവേശിക്കാമെന്ന് നിങ്ങൾ വിചാരിക്കുന്നുവെങ്കിൽ, അതുവേണ്ട": അതുവേണ്ട is literally "that is not wanted / no need of that", a dismissal of the thought, not a forecast. "if you think that you can enter Paradise, that is not to be." → "… that will not do."

### Renderings not as settled in earlier parts

4. പുണ്യപുരുഷൻമാർ (v. 146 panel "[പുണ്യ പുരുഷൻമാർ]", its table "രിബ്ബീകൾ (പുണ്യപുരുഷൻമാർ)"): Al-Baqarah part 13 (its new-terms table and p. 307, Ma'idah 44) rendered പുണ്യപുരുഷന്മാർ "pious men". "‘ribbi’s [men of merit]" → "‘ribbi’s [pious men]"; table "ribbis (men of merit)" → "ribbis (pious men)". പുണ്യവാൻമാർ (p. 867, twice) stays "meritorious ones" (Al-Baqarah parts 27, 31: പുണ്യവാൻ "the meritorious one").
5. വമ്പിച്ച: p. 864 "വമ്പിച്ച പ്രതിഫലം" is "a huge reward" and p. 867 "വമ്പിച്ച പ്രതിഫലങ്ങളും" was "great rewards"; one rendering is now used for the one word in this part (part 11 also has "huge punishment"). "In the next world He gave great rewards too." → "In the next world He gave huge rewards too." (Al-Baqarah parts 08–16 use "mighty" for വമ്പിച്ച with عظيم; see open doubt 6.)

No dropped തന്നെ/-ഉം or list items, may/will errors other than correction 1, voice changes, reversed or "fixed" negations, changed reported speech, added ﷺ, silently corrected names or misprints, or added or re-vowelled Arabic were found.
