## The translator's open items

- **(1) DOUBT verse 21, "(വോ)".** Clearly printed "ചെയ്യുന്നു(വോ)-", the bracket holding only the relative ending that closes the യാതൊരു കൂട്ടർ … -വോ clause before "അവർക്ക്". The English "the people who: …" already carries the relative sense, so any English inside the bracket is either a loose equivalent ("(whoever they are)", as part 01 rendered the word (ആരോ) in verse 4) or a re-wording ("(those who) do (so)"). The page cannot settle which the reviewer will want: DOUBT kept as it is.
- **(2) TN verse 19, ആരെങ്കിലും twice.** Confirmed: the panel (p. 729) reads "… ആരെങ്കിലും അവിശ്വസിക്കുന്നപക്ഷം ആരെങ്കിലും എന്നാൽ, (അറിഞ്ഞുകൊള്ളട്ടെ) …"; the word table (entry 122) has it once. TN kept.
- **(3) TN p. 730, "എന്നാൽ ലഭിക്കുകയും …".** Confirmed: "എന്നാൽ" ends a justified line and the next line begins "ലഭിക്കുകയും"; no object is printed (amanithafseer.com has the same). TN kept.
- **(4) Slips kept as printed without TN.** All confirmed on the images and the text layer: ക്ലപനാ (p. 727, for കല്പനാ; "commands"); കൺകാഴചയിൽ (p. 724 commentary, no virama under ഴ; the verse 13 panel and table entry 42 have കൺകാഴ്ചയിൽ); സൂക്ഷമത (table entry 73, p. 726; the verse 15 panel has സൂക്ഷ്മത); വിവരമറിയിക്കട്ടയോ (table entry 70); എനി (verse 20 panel, for ഇനി); "വിചാരണനടത്തുന്ന വനാകുന്നു" (verse 19 panel, the last line of the panel, not justified, so the gap is real). - **(5) Quran quotations in the commentary.** p. 724: printed **يرونهم مثليهم رأي العين** = exactly the words يَرَوۡنَهُم مِّثۡلَيۡهِمۡ رَأۡيَ ٱلۡعَيۡنِ of 3:13, no more and no less. p. 726: printed **قل من حرم زينة الله** = exactly قُلۡ مَنۡ حَرَّمَ زِينَةَ ٱللَّهِ, the opening of 7:32 (the author's Malayalam then renders the rest of the verse's first sentence). The print differs only in script (plain, unvowelled), so no TN is needed. Both are copied from `data/quran-uthmani.json` as rule 4 says; note that part 01 kept the printed script instead (as Al-Baqarah parts 39–51), so the two parts differ in method (open doubt 3). `npm run check` counts all 16 longer quotations: 11 exact, 0 close-but-not-exact, 5 not from the Quran (part 01's four and this part's hadith phrase).
