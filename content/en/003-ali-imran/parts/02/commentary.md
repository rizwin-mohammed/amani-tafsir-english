### Section – 2

*[Verses 10–12]*

*[p. 723]* It is at a time when the whole of the Arabian peninsula had not yet come under subjection to Islam that Allah warns the disbelievers that you will be conquered, and that thus victory will come to hand for the true believers . This is a good sign of the truthfulness of both the Quran and the Prophet ﷺ. The famous event that marked the beginning of the defeat of the disbelievers is, surely, the battle of Badr; pointing to it, Allah says:-

*[Verse 13]*

*[p. 724]* Without any preparation at all, more than three hundred and ten true believers who carried only the swords that the Arabs used habitually to use in those times, and about a thousand mushriks who had come prepared with as much war equipment as possible: the battle famous in history that took place between them is indeed the battle of Badr. It is the great sign contained in the unprecedented victory that the true believers obtained in it that Allah reminds of. That that small band, who had to go out to battle in the way of Allah inspired by true faith, was able to defeat utterly that big band, who clashed with them maddened in the intoxication of disbelief, was only by the help of Allah, surely. The gist is that those who have far-sightedness and firmness of mind can understand, surely, that in future too Allah will keep making the true believers victorious, and that thus the deniers of the truth will become completely defeated.

**يَرَوۡنَهُم مِّثۡلَيۡهِمۡ رَأۡيَ ٱلۡعَيۡنِ** (In eyesight- or in outward sight - they saw them as twice them): concerning what is meant by the pronoun ( **ضمير** )s in this sentence, the Quran commentators have differing opinions. Among them, the two relevant opinions are these.

(1) To the disbelievers, the true believers seemed, to outward sight, to be twice themselves- or about two thousand-.

(2) To the disbelievers, the true believers seemed to be twice their real number, or more than six hundred. Either way, the gist will be that, although the number of the true believers was in reality very small, in the sight of the mushriks they seemed to be many. ‘When you clashed with each other, Allah showed them as few in your sight, and you as few in their sight.’ Allah has stated thus in Su: Anfal 44. As has been made clear in some riwayahs, these two are said with reference to two occasions. Allah at first made it seem so both to the mushriks, so that, thinking that the Muslims were very few, they would face them eagerly, and to the Muslims, so that, thinking that the mushriks were not many, they would clash with them boldly. It is about that that was stated in Anfal. Afterwards, when they clashed on the battlefield, in order that the morale of the enemies would be destroyed and they would meet defeat, the Muslims seemed in their sight to be very many. It is about this that was stated here.

*[Verses 14–15]*

*[p. 726]* **قنطار** (qintar)'s plural form is **قناطير** (qanatir). Heap, pile: such are its meanings. It is also used for a measure of weight that comes to more or less a hundred ratls. That word is also usually used meaning wealth in plenty. Here too that is what is meant. All the things mentioned above, beginning with women, are ones that seem very important to men. They regard love for them as an adornment for themselves. In reality all of them are only temporary and perishable worldly pleasures. In real fact, it is not for them that man ought to have love. It is heavenly favours, ever so much higher and more imperishable than them, that are with Allah. Above everything is indeed the pleasure of Allah. It is towards all these that he ought to show love. It is for them, too, that he ought to strive: that is the gist.

The import of what has been said is not that children, wives and the like, and riches, must not be made use of at all. To set an excessive value on them. To give them precedence over the favours of the Hereafter, to forget the matter of the Hereafter fixing attention on them and immersing in them: it is these that are reproached. If one shows gratitude to Allah in the pleasures that have come to hand, and does not let it bring a shaking to the awareness about Allah and the Hereafter, they will not be harmful. On the contrary, they will also be beneficial. Allah says..... **قُلۡ مَنۡ حَرَّمَ زِينَةَ ٱللَّهِ** (Say: Who is it that has made forbidden the adornment that Allah has produced for His servants, and the excellent articles of food?! (A'raf:32)

Who all are the ones who give importance to material comforts and conveniences, who make them the aim of life, who misuse them, who all are the ones who handle them in a moderate and lawful manner, who set a value on the favours of the Hereafter and on the pleasure of Allah above them: all such matters Allah knows truly. He will also give each one the recompense that he deserves: it is such things that He alerts to through the last sentence ‘Allah is the One who sees and knows about His servants’. It was said, surely, that it is those who *[p. 727]* observe caution who obtain favours such as Paradise. The special characteristics of those who observe caution are described in the next verse:-

*[Verses 16–17]*

In the supplication We have believed, therefore do forgive us, and do protect us from punishment- in having spoken of the forgiveness of sins and the deliverance from punishment in connection with true faith- a fundamental principle is contained. Only those who have accepted true faith will obtain forgiveness of sins and salvation. That only they have the worthiness to request both of them: that is it. If they are those who offer humility and lowliness to Allah through supplications like these, they ought to have, surely, some special qualities too that make them fit for it. Those qualities are these:

(1) **Patience:** that is, enduring all the difficulties that are met in observing commands and directions and in abandoning prohibitions, keeping patience in calamities and in unpleasant things, and so on.

(2) **Truth:** that is, keeping to truth in intention, and in deed, and in word

(3) **Devotion:** or obeying Allah with humility and with respect.

(4) **Spending:** Obligatory charities, and generously *[p. 728]* using wealth in good works, are included in this

(5) **Supplication for forgiveness of sins:** supplicating for forgiveness of sins in the last times of the night. That this time is a time when supplications get an answer specially, and that it is a time more special for the prayer and for deeds such as dhikr, is widely known from the Quran and from the sayings of the Prophet.

*[Verse 18]*

That Allah alone is the one object of worship who has the worthiness of worship, and that all other objects of worship are illusions and the unworthy: this reality Allah has established clearly through ever so many signs and proofs. The angels and those who have knowledge, all of them, have understood it and have firmly believed it. The One who keeps up and goes on conducting the order of justice of the entire universe is none other than He. He is the Majestic, who is invincible, and the Sagacious, who is perfect in philosophic wisdom. The import is that, therefore, only He has the worthiness to be the object of worship. It has come in Hadiths that the Prophet ﷺ used to say, when reciting this verse, ‘O Rabb, I too am a witness to it ( **وانا على ذلك من الشاهدين يارب** ). From its having been said that those who have knowledge too have borne witness that there is no object of worship except Allah, one matter can be understood. That is indeed that people who do not bear witness to the existence and the oneness of Allah -even if they are extremely capable in some other subjects- will be, with Allah, mere ignorant ones and fools.

*[Verses 19–20]*

*[p. 730]* As was explained in Suratul Fatihah, the word Din ( **الدين** ) can come to have many meanings according to the context, such as procedure, custom, legal proceedings, obedience, habit, religion.It is in the meaning religion that it is mostly used. Here too that is what is meant. Religion is a system of life- or a system of law that ought to be adopted in life for the pleasure of God-. But - as some are used to supposing- that is not the meaning of the word ‘Din’. Because trade is an occupation, the meaning of the word ‘trade’ will not come to be occupation, surely.

The one religion that Allah appointed for all, from the beginning of man to the end of man, and that was being preached, beginning from the first prophet and the first scripture, up to the last prophet and the holy Quran, which is the last scripture, and that Allah has been pleased to grant to mankind, is Islam. It is to it alone that He has called men at all times, and that He keeps calling them. As comes in the 85th verse, no other religion than it is acceptable with Him either. But some communities that received *[TN: what they received is not printed; a word seems to have dropped out after എന്നാൽ (“But”).]* and that claim the heritage of being followers of the prophets have divided away from this truth. The reason for that is not their not having received enough knowledge. It is only characteristics of defiance, such as envy, hatred, enmity, excessive ambition, that came about among them even after getting knowledge. Though this is the fact, what they do is to carry on dispute and argument, contrary to this truth, even about tawhid, which is the supreme foundation of Islam. There is no use in carrying on debate with them. Therefore Allah commands the Prophet ﷺ that there is no need to stand for dispute with them, and that it is enough to say that I and the true believers who follow me, believing in the oneness of Allah and worshipping Him alone, have become submissive to Him, and keep away.

Whether they are the People of the Scripture or the illiterate Arabs who have not become acquainted with people of scriptures, the Prophet ﷺ needs only to convey to all the preaching of Islam and to call them to it. To make them all Muslims is not the liability of the Prophet ﷺ. Allah will carry out the reckoning and the decision of those who, even after receiving the preaching and the call, do not become submissive. He has no difficulty at all in it. He keeps seeing and knowing the state of each one. He also alerts the Prophet ﷺ thus. In a Hadith narrated from Abu Hurairah (r) *[p. 731]* the Prophet ﷺ has said thus: ‘By Him indeed in whose possession my soul is, whoever at all of this human community - be it a Jew, be it a Christian - hears about me and does not believe in that with which I have been sent, he will not fail to be of the people of Hell,’ (Mu.)

### Section – 3

*[Verses 21–22]*

It is about some defiances and corruptions that the People of the Scripture went on practising that it speaks. They have indeed a long heritage in denying the evidences of Allah, which are the words of scripture and the signs, in mercilessly killing the prophets and religious counsellors, and so on. We saw many particulars concerning this in Suratul *[p. 732]* Baqarah. Allah warns them that, in case they do not return from that old heritage to true faith, not only will they have to suffer an extremely severe punishment, but, if they have done anything as good deeds, there is no benefit at all for them to obtain by it, be it in this world, be it in the next world, and there is no help at all for them to expect, and so on. In case they do not accept true faith, they will not, because of those deeds, become worthy of any praise or fame or favourable treatment in this world. Nor will they obtain any meritorious reward in the Hereafter: that is the gist.

In the place where ‘warn’ about punishment ought to be said, having said ‘give glad tidings’ is a figurative usage in the form of mockery. The import is that, if there is any glad tidings at all to announce to them, it is only about punishment.
