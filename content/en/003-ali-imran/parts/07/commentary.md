*[Verse 58]*

The gist is that all the information stated above is what Allah sends down to the Prophet ﷺ as evidences sufficient for the truthfulness of the noble Prophet ﷺ, for the People of the Scripture to understand the true state concerning 'Isa (a), and as the admonitions of the Quran. Although the true state in the matter of 'Isa (a) is just as described above, if anyone thinks that, because of the special feature that occurred in his birth, he ought, surely, to have some special ranks of position that have not come to any other human beings, Allah gives the reply to that:-

*[Verses 59–60]*

*[p. 777]* It was not from a father, or from a mother, that Allah created and made Adam (a), the first father of the human race and the first human being. Nor had there been any contact of woman and man before that. Allah formed and made him from soil. Then He commanded it to become a human being. It became a human being. That is all. As for the birth of 'Isa, it is from a mother. That being so, how much more novel and wonderful than that is the origin of Adam. That being the case, is it not to Adam that a higher position than 'Isa ought to be ascribed? No one ascribes divinity to him. The gist is that, the reality being this, there is no room in this matter for doubt or for confusion of ideas. It is not because there is a possibility of the Prophet ﷺ doubting; on the contrary, this is a warning that others must not become subject to doubt.

Without male and female Allah created Adam (a). He created Hawwa' (a) too, not through a female. He created 'Isa (a) too, without the connection of a male. It is from a male and from a female that the creation of all the others goes on taking place. Then, that Allah is capable of doing whatever He intends, and that as regards Him *[p. 778]*, there is no such thing as a law of nature that must not be changed, is clear indeed. Apart from the fact that we, taking as the basis some relations of cause and effect that we usually go on seeing and becoming familiar with, regard such-and-such as a law of nature, and that, if anything happens contrary to it, it astonishes us, as far as Allah, the Creator of that natural order, is concerned, everything is just alike. If He intends that a thing should come into being, He need only command that it should come into being. It just comes into being Allah continues:-

*[Verses 61–63]*

*[p. 779]* That the real history of the Prophet 'Isa (a) is in such-and-such a manner, and that he is not God or the son of God, was described in detail in the past verses. It was also pointed out that there is no further room for doubt in it. What is seen in the 61st verse is that Allah - as a final stroke, as it were - calls to a ‘Mubahalah ( **مباهلة** )’ the Christians who, even after having heard and learnt all that and having been unable to present proofs against it, stand firm in disbelief itself, not ready to return to the truth. To perform a prayer of curse against one another from the depths of the heart - that is, that both parties gather together and all pray to Allah from the bottom of the heart, ‘May the curse of Allah come upon those among us who are false claimants’, that indeed is what is meant by ‘Mubahalah’.

We saw before, surely, that more or less eighty verses from the beginning of this surah - or else some verses - came down following the coming of the Christian deputation that came from Najran, and that the Prophet ﷺ in the end invited them to Mubahalah, and that what they did was to back away. It was according to this verse that the Prophet ﷺ invited them to it. At first they did agree to it. Taking along Ali, Fatimah, Hasan, Husain (r) as well, the Prophet ﷺ came ready at the fixed time and place. But, the Christian band did not dare to come. What they did was, for the time being, to withdraw, giving some excuses, and, making some treaty settlements with the Prophet ﷺ, *[p. 780]* to go back. When some leaders of the band showed themselves in favour of the ‘Mubahalah’, some others did say to them thus. ‘You know that Muhammad is a truthful prophet. A people that performs a prayer of curse ( **مباهلة** ) with a prophet will not fail to be reduced to a name only.’ About them the Prophet ﷺ said: ‘If they had set out and come to the Mubahalah, they would have returned without their property and family (having lost everything).’ The said event can be seen recorded by the scholars of hadith, in detail and in brief, through many routes.

On occasions of debate, even if the truth dawns clearly, it is usual for many, because of arrogance and obstinacy, not to be ready to accept the truth. They will also stand firm in their own position, using any rash deed and tricks. But, no one who has a little intellect will dare to drag his own children and women all together into ruin. It may be for that reason that it is commanded to make them too take part in the Mubahalah. **الله اعلم**. For those who have full conviction that the truth is on their side there is, moreover, nothing at all to fear. What is contained in the 63rd verse is the warning that those who, having been convinced that the truth is such-and-such, still do not accept it, and back away from the Mubahalah, are corrupters and makers of disorder, and that Allah knows about them as is due, and that He will in due course take the necessary measures against them.

### Section – 7

*[Verse 64]*

*[p. 781]* It is the communities that keep up the tradition of being the followers of the prophets that are called **أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ** (People of the Scripture). Those existing as People of the Scripture are the Jews and the Christians. There is no Rasul and no scripture that has not preached that other than Allah must not be worshipped . and that no partners and equals at all must be set up for Him. Allah says: **وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا نُوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ** ( **الانبياء: ٢٥** ) (We have not sent any Rasul before you except by giving wahy that there is not any Ilah other than Me, therefore you must worship Me. (Anbiya') Therefore Allah commands the Prophet ﷺ to invite them to that fundamental doctrine which all the People of the Scripture are alike obliged to accept. Although the People of the Scripture have more or less deviated from that foundation, which is tawhid, the claim of all of them is that they are the bearers of its mission.

Although the meaning of the word **كلمة** is ‘word’, it is also usually used meaning more than one word, or sentences, or discourses. The word ‘word’ in Malayalam too is usually used like this, surely. It is according to this that it was said about the principles mentioned above, ‘a word equal between us and you’. It can be seen in the hadiths and in the history books that, according to this command, the Prophet ﷺ did on many occasions invite the Christians and the Jews - by means of writing and otherwise -. The letter that the Prophet ﷺ sent to Heraclius, the Roman emperor, is indeed specially worth mentioning among these. What Allah has specially mentioned here are three fundamental principles that all who are People of the Scripture are obliged to agree to and to concur in *[p. 782]*. That is:-

(1) Not to worship other than Allah. Once this principle has been truly accepted, it becomes impossible to offer to anyone else other than Allah any matter at all that falls within the category of worship, and that comes about in mind, in word or in deed.

(2) Not to associate anything with Him. Every belief, and word, and deed that makes out that anyone has any share, whether in His attributes, or in His rights of authority, or in His actions or His essence, is associating partners with Him. It is this, too, that is called shirk.

(3) For some not to make others Rabbs. That is, not to ascribe the position of Rabb to anyone else other than Allah. The expression ‘some of us making others Rabb’ is meaningful. A hint is contained in it that, when all human beings are creations and servants of Allah, there is no justification at all, surely, for some among human beings to make and keep others as Rabbs. In reality, while this matter is included in the first two principles, that it has been specially mentioned is because this matter needs to be specially heeded. Man's worshipping man, and obeying the command of others against the command of Allah, and ascribing divinity to human individuals, all these are indeed each a kind of making Rabb. But, there is also another making Rabb that is more hidden than all that, and more far-reaching than that. That is:-

To make and keep scholars and priests as Rabbs. It is about this that Allah has said in Suratut Tawbah: **ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَحۡبَارَهُمۡ وَرُهۡبَٰنَهُمۡ أَرۡبَابٗا مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ**’ (They - the People of the Scripture - made their scholars and priests Rabbs (9: 31). 'Adiyy ibn Hatim (r) was a Christian before he came into Islam. He asked the Prophet ﷺ about this sentence. He said: ‘We do not worship them, surely! (That being so, what is the meaning of this? ) ‘The Prophet ﷺ replied: ‘Did they not make some things lawful for you, and make some things forbidden for you, and did you not accept that?’ He agreed: ‘Yes’. The noble one said: ‘That itself is it.’ (Ti)

To observe as real religious laws the laws that the Christian priests make up from time to time is the custom of the Christians up to this day. This is a fact that everyone knows. Even an idiom, ‘What the padre said is religion for the Nasrani’, has come about from this. No need; examine the state of the Muslims themselves! If any one particular scholar or great man says or opines something about a subject, even if it is clear that it is against Allah's Quran and the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ *[p. 783]*, the practice of accepting it, making it a religious ruling, for the sole reason that he said it, is a great disease that has spread and taken hold in the Muslim community. It can be said that the fortunate ones who have escaped from this disease are very few. If only the masses who are not ready to enquire what the true state is, or to think about the evidence, or to accept the reality if they understand it, and the scholars who lead them astray and exploit them, would just take to heart this Quran verse and the saying of the Prophet! The principle that those who lack knowledge must ask and learn from those who have knowledge, and must accept their word, is indeed right. But, what is the danger is blind imitation, and not turning back even when the evidence is understood.

If, in the matter of inviting the People of the Scripture to tawhid by describing the circumstances of the Prophet 'Isa (a), the statements of this surah from the beginning are reviewed, it can be seen - as Imam Baidawi (r) has pointed out - that what Allah has adopted is a very fitting and just order of policy. First He described the origin of 'Isa (a), and his circumstances, and his unworthiness to be God. Then, He exposed the hollowness of the superstitions and the confusions of ideas about him that the Christians keep hidden in their minds. When they would not give up dispute and obstinacy, He called them to Mubahalah. When He saw that, although their strength of mind was injured by it, they were not coming forward for it, He placed and showed before them a very light way. Yes, He tried inviting them to a foundation on which both parties can agree. When He saw that signs, and arguments and warnings, and counsels, none at all of them, were taking effect - lastly - Allah commands the Prophet ﷺ to leave them to themselves, saying ‘Bear witness that here we are, Muslims who have become submissive to Allah’. That is, the gist is: if your intention is to continue as disbelievers without becoming submissive to Him and becoming Muslims, you may do so; we are not ready for that.

A general principle can be understood from this verse: that if groups who live in ideological difference of opinion with one another go along together on the eternal values on which all can agree together and about which there is no difference of opinion between them, it will help the estrangement between the parties gradually to disappear. **والله اعلم**

*[Verses 65–67]*

*[p. 785]* The Prophet Ibrahim (a) is, surely, a person whom the Jews and the Christians respect and honour. What both parties claim is that he was a man of their religion, and a person who had adopted their procedures. Muhammad ibn Ishaq (r) has narrated as a riwayah that, on the occasion when the Christian deputation of Najran came, they and the Jews together did hold dispute and controversy on this subject in the presence of the Prophet ﷺ. It is about this dispute between the two parties that Allah reproaches both parties in one voice. The Jews are the followers of the Torah. The Christians are the followers of the Injil. It is long after the time of Ibrahim (a) that the sending down of both those books took place. That being so, it is clear, surely, that the claim of both parties is hollow and meaningless. Allah points out that what both parties are doing is the foolishness of holding dispute and argument on a subject that they do not know.

What they have is a tradition of carrying on dispute and argument with one another even on subjects concerning the scripture and the prophet of both parties. It may be said that those disputes are about a subject that they know. But, asking, what right do they have to hold dispute about the Prophet Ibrahim (a), who passed away so long ago, and about his way? and alerting them that it is Allah who knows the true state - you do not know -, Allah describes that his real position was such-and-such. Yes, he was not a Jew, or a Christian, nor was he a person belonging, (like the Arabs), to the company of the mushriks. It has been made clear that he was one pure of heart who adopted no crooked way at all, and a Muslim who, truly believing in Allah, lived on wholly submissive to Him. The fact being this, in the next verse it is also pointed out who it is that has the worthiness to claim a close connection with the Prophet Ibrahim (a):-

*[Verse 68]*

*[p. 786]* Only those, whoever they are, who have kept on following Ibrahim (a), taking him as a model, in the way that he adopted and in his procedures, have the means to claim closeness and connection with him. Let them be people of whatever time or of whatever group; such indeed are this prophet, the Prophet Muhammad, and the true believers who have believed in him. The purport is that, because you, both parties who are People of the Scripture, have gone very far away from his way, you have come to have no connection with him.
