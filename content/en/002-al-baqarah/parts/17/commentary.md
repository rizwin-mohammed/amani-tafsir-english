### Section – 14

*\[Verse 113\]*

*\[p. 345\]* Although both groups have agreement in a few certain matters, what there is in reality between the Jews and the Christians is sworn enmity. The Jews argue that the Christians have no basis at all and no authority, and, quite contrary, the Christians likewise argue about the Jews. Both groups, moreover, have been reading through the scripture. The Jews, who are the people of the Torah, know the necessity of believing in the prophets who come later, and the prediction about the coming of 'Isa (a). The Christians, who are the people of the Injil, also know that 'Isa (a) said that it is not to deny the former prophets – it is to strengthen them – that he has come. It is without heeding any of this that each group mutually denies the very basis of the others. That being so, it is, surely, no wonder that the people who know not about the scripture too argue like this. In any case, on the Day of Qiyamah Allah will not remain without bringing all the sections to reckoning and taking a decision. Then it will be known which is the party of truth, whose argument is right, and so on.

That it was with the Arab mushriks in view that **ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ** (those who do not know) was said is what most of the commentators say. Some say that it was with those before the Torah in view. The opinion that Ibn Jarir (r) approved is that, not with a particular section in view, all who have not been connected with scriptures will be included in it. It is this, too, that seems better. It is indeed an argument seen among many communities and religions, both of earlier times and of later times – the argument that all the others are without basis, that they alone are the ones with basis. This belief can be seen among many castes in India too. The warning at the end of this verse is indeed applicable to all. Yes, on the Day of Qiyamah Allah will carry out judgement in the matter of all.

*\[Verse 114\]*

*\[p. 346\]* The plural of **مَسْجِدٌ** (masjid) is **مَسَاجِد** . ‘The place where sujud is done’ is the word meaning. It is the name for the mosque (Muslim mosque)s that are built for worshipping Allah alone. What is meant by His name being remembered – or being celebrated – in them is acts of worship such as the prayer, supplication and the like. For the word **خَرَاب** (kharab) the meaning is emptiness, going to waste, ruin and so on. Even if, in a house, it comes about that there is no one dwelling, or that it is unfit to use, and even if damage comes to it by its being pulled down or otherwise, they will say about that house **بَيْت خَرَاب** (a house that is kharab).

That creating obstruction to its being carried out in the mosques that are established for worshipping Allah, and for His name to be remembered and to be celebrated, and, without permitting it, trying to make them empty, is the greatest wrongdoing among wrongdoings, and that the doers of wrong who do so have no right to enter the mosques fearlessly – is made known in this verse. It also warns that they will have to experience disgrace in this world, and mighty punishment in the next. This verse is not one that refers to only some one particular mosque. The idea contained in it is indeed one that affects all mosques. That those who bear witness in disbelief have no right to maintain and to protect mosques, and that only those who believe in Allah and in the Last Day, and observe the prayer and Zakat, *\[p. 347\]* and fear Allah alone, have the right to it, Allah has made clear in the verses Su:Tawbah 17,18 too. More detail can be seen there.

Although it is about mosques as a whole that the ayah states, when its sentence style is looked at, an indication can be seen in it that some event in which a mosque was obstructed has taken place. Variance of opinion can be seen as to which this event is. That the Quraish stopped them when the Prophet ﷺ and the Companions, intending the 'umrah rite, set out from Madinah and reached Hudaibiyyah, near the Haram of Makkah, and that in the end the famous Hudaibiya treaty took place, and that they returned for the time being and the 'umrah rite was performed the next year, is, surely, famous in history. The disgrace that those wrongdoers experienced in this world is also well known. Some say that it is this event that is behind this. There are other opinions too, such as that it indicates the Christians, earlier, out of the hatred they had for the Jews, committing wrongdoing in Bait al-Muqaddas and stopping the Jews. Some criticisms too have been raised about each opinion. At the same time, some are also of the opinion that it is not an event that had taken place that is indicated in it, and that what is in it is an indication concerning some events that may happen in the future. (Allah knows) Whichever it was, the idea and the import of the ayah are indeed clear.

Everyone knows that, in the name of some splits of opinion, some events have been taking place in these times in the midst of the community in which the faction among the Muslims of a region that has the majority and influential power does not permit the other faction to enter the mosques, or keeps creating obstruction to it. It is mostly against the section that opposes superstitions and improper customs – they will, surely, most often be the minority – that this wrongdoing is wont to be carried out. Such people too must specially take this verse to heart.

*\[Verse 115\]*

*\[p. 348\]* The places of rising and setting – the east and the west, all – are Allah's. Yes, every direction is in Allah's ownership and in His knowledge and control. He has no special closeness and connection with any direction or with any region. Therefore, it is not that only if it is done turning towards such-and-such a direction will His regard and attention be in the acts of worship and the supplications made to Him. He is not one who is limited to some place or direction. He is Vast and All-Knowing. A deed done turning in whatever direction will be included in His knowledge and attention – that is the gist.

That a particular direction is fixed as qiblah is not because Allah is in that direction, or because He has a special connection with that direction. It is aiming at many aims such as the unification of the community, convenience and the like. The Prophet ﷺ, in the period of his life in Makkah, had adopted the Ka'bah as qiblah according to the custom of the Quraish. After coming to Madinah, the noble Prophet kept adopting as qiblah Bait al-Muqaddas, which had been the qiblah of the Jews. After sixteen or seventeen months, as is seen in the verses below, it was commanded to turn again to the Ka'bah itself. The Jews spread around many things about these changes, and made it a weapon to create confusions among the Muslims. There was murmuring among the Arabs too about having made Bait al-Muqaddas qiblah. This verse is one that is as a reply to all this.

The intent of having said **فَأَيۡنَمَا تُوَلُّواْ فَثَمَّ وَجۡهُ ٱللَّهِ** (wherever you turn, there is Allah's face) is not that His face will be situated in the direction in which you turn, or that He is omnipresent, being situated in every place. This conception is likening Allah to creatures, and reckoning Him by the criterion by which creatures are reckoned **لَيۡسَ كَمِثۡلِهِۦ شَيۡءٞ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ** (There is not any thing like Him. He indeed is the One who knows all, hearing and seeing) No place at all where He is not will be left vacant.( **لا يخلو منه مكان** ) – about some being seen to have stated so, Ibn Kathir(r) says: If it is in the sense that Allah's knowledge is everywhere that it has been said, it is right. His knowledge has encompassed the universe. Now, if it is in the sense that Allah's essence is situated in His creatures, it is not right. **تعال الله عن ذلك علوا كبيرا** (Allah is very far exalted above all that) In fact, for those with intelligence to understand the import of that statement, it will suffice to heed its very next sentence. Yes, Allah is Vast and *\[p. 349\]* All-Knowing ( **إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٞ** ) – that last sentence.

When such things as Allah's face ( **وَجۡهُ ٱللَّهِ** ) and Allah's hand ( **يَدُ اللهِ** ) are seen used in the Quran or in hadith, the habit of the great men who are predecessor ( **سلف** )s is to say, without giving any interpretations of their own to any of them, that we know the meaning of the word – that we do not know what its reality is. They will believe in it and leave it, holding that Allah knows the reality. The habit of many of the successor ( **خلف** )s is to give them some interpretation suited to the context. The aim of both groups is indeed not to assign to those words a meaning that suggests in any way a likeness of Allah to creatures. The safer way, moreover, is that of the predecessors. If the style and the context of the Quran verses and of the Prophet's sayings in which such words, which suggest a likeness to the qualities of creatures when the outward meaning is looked at, have been used are examined, indeed – even though it may perhaps be difficult to define in the form of words – it will also be possible for each one, more or less, according to his own state, to understand that their gist is such-and-such. **ومن الله التوفيق**

*\[Verses 116–117\]*

*\[p. 350\]* There are many parties that argue that Allah has offspring. It can be said that it is the Christians who stand in the front line. The very foundation stone of the Christian religion is that 'Isa (a) is the son of God. The Jews have the argument that 'Uzair (a) ( **عزير** ) is the son of Allah, and the Arab mushriks that the angels are the daughters of Allah. Like this, the Hindus and others too have been imagining some individuals to be offspring of God. It can be seen that Allah has reproached this argument in many places in the Quran in words far fuller of indignation and seriousness than what is in this verse. That He does not take punitive action, in this very world, against those who raise this argument, which does not befit the exalted greatness and the supreme holiness of Allah, and which is very greatly wrath-provoking to Him, is indeed the vastness of His mercy. It may be as indicating that, that in many places that state about this argument (19:88, 91:92, 21:26, 43:17,81) *\[DOUBT: "91:92" is printed clearly. In the Quran text used here (data/quran-uthmani.json) Surah 91 has only 15 verses, so the reference cannot be found as printed; 19:91 and 19:92 there also concern the claim of offspring and use the name الرحمن. Translated as printed; please confirm.\]* the name the Most Compassionate ( **الرَّحْمَن** ), which, among the excellent names of Allah, deserves very great importance, is seen used. **الله أعلم** Allah has pointed out in these verses four or five realities that make clear the hollowness of this utterly base and vile argument.

(1) **سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ** (He is Supremely Holy!) – that itself. Besides its being a word that indicates wonder, when the breadth of idea contained in it is looked at, it can be seen that all the other refutations of this argument are contained in it. When He is supremely holy from all the qualities of creatures, and perfect and supremely exalted in all excellent qualities, how is it that He has children like creatures ?! – that is the import.

(2) **لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ** (all that is in the heavens and the earth is His) all are what He created. What stand in His ownership, according to His control and authority. In none of it has He a spouse, or a companion, or helpers, or partners. Not to speak of 'Isa, or 'Uzair, or the angels, or ‘goddesses and gods’, not a single thing – is exempt from this. That being so, how will some of His creatures become His offspring?! **أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لَهُۥ وَلَدٞ وَلَمۡ تَكُن لَّهُۥ صَٰحِبَةٞ وَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ** (How will He have offspring?! He has no companion at all; He has also created everything; He, for His part, is also the One who knows about every thing – that being so! (An'am 101)

*\[p. 351\]* (3) **كُلّٞ لَّهُۥ قَٰنِتُونَ** (all are submissive to Him)All are subject to the arrangements of law that He decreed, to the rules of nature, and to His intentions. That being so, how do only some, being exempt from it, reach the position of His progeny and equality with Him ?! Children, surely, will be ones with the nature of the race of their parents. Here, for its part, there is no such thing as race, moreover.

(4) **بَدِيعُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ** (He is the One who created and brought into being the heavens and the earth without a model) Yes, without another model, from sheer nothingness, He created and brought into being. Apart from everything being what He made and brought into being, there is nothing born from Him. Nor is He born from anything else. Is there anything at all equal to Him ? That too there is not ( **لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ** **وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ** )

(5) **وَإِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا....الخ** (When He decides on a matter, He need only say to it ‘come into being’. It will come into being) Small and big, living and non-living, all are equal in this. That being so, there is no justification at all for saying that, excepting some from it, they are ones who have taken on existence as His offspring. Arguing that Allah has offspring, closing the eyes towards all these realities – what else is it but reviling Allah and running down His supreme exaltedness and supreme holiness ?!

As stated by the Prophet ﷺ, Ibn 'Abbas (r) quotes : Allah says : ‘The son of Adam (man) has treated Me as a liar. That was not proper for him. He has also reviled Me. That too was not proper for him. As for his treating Me as a liar, it is his saying that I cannot bring him back (a second time) as he had (first) been. As for his reviling Me, it is his saying that I have offspring. From taking a female companion or offspring, how very holy I am ! (Bukhari) In another saying of the Prophet it has come thus : There is indeed no one else at all who, having heard disturbing words, is more forbearing about it (without taking action) than Allah. They (the disbelievers) ascribe offspring to Him. He, for His part, gives them food and gives them health (Bu; Mu.)

*\[Verse 118\]*

It is not that it cannot be in the sense of fools too. Muhammad, if all that you say is true, why can Allah not say it to us directly ?! Why must it indeed be through your medium?! Before too, many – the Israelites and others – have demanded thus of their respective prophets. In evil obstinacy, denial of the truth, defiance and the like, the mentality of all is of the same kind. *\[Verse 119\]*

The two sides of the preaching of the prophets are announcing glad tidings and announcing tidings of fear. That is, along with explaining the truth and the straight way, announcing good news about the gains and merit-rewards that are to be obtained through it; and, explaining untruth and the evil way, giving forewarning and warning about the consequences and punishments that are to be faced through it. This is the charge of the prophets. With that their task is fulfilled. Accepting truth and rejecting untruth is the duty of each individual. The prophets have no obligation in it. They will not be asked why such-and-such persons did not believe, or did not become ones on right guidance. Allah says: **فَإِنَّمَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ وَعَلَيۡنَا ٱلۡحِسَابُ** – الرعد: ٤٠ ‘Only preaching is your obligation, the reckoning is Our obligation’ – that is the gist.
