*[Verses 161–162]*

It will be with his death that whether a person is a true believer or a disbeliever is finally determined. If a person who was a disbeliever for his whole lifetime accepts true belief at the end, and dies at that very moment indeed, he becomes one among the true believers. Conversely, if a true believer, at the moment just before death, abandons that belief and accepts disbelief, he also comes to be in the company of the disbelievers. That is why, when stating about the curse, without stopping at saying ‘upon those who disbelieved’, it has been said ‘upon those who also died as disbelievers’. So, it can be understood from this that, because a person has been seen as a disbeliever, it is not permitted to curse him, and that only if it has become known that he died as a disbeliever is it permitted to curse him. The reason for the scholars saying that, although disbelievers, as a whole, *[p. 420]* may be cursed, it is not permitted to curse particular individuals who are disbelievers, is that how that individual's end culminated will not be known to anyone other than Allah. Only by wahy will even the prophets be able to ascertain it. Even then, other than that that individual may be cursed, it is not reckoned as a good deed either. Many sentences cursing in general the disbelievers, and the wrongdoers, the false claimants and the like, can be seen in the Quran. But Quran sentences cursing individuals other than the devil are not seen. That, on the occasion of punishing a man who had drunk liquor many times, one who was present there said: May Allah curse him, how very many times it is that he has drunk!; and that, on hearing it, the Prophet ﷺ said: Do not become a help to the devil against your brother – has come in hadith. (Bu.Mu)

While there are among mankind both believers and disbelievers, how does the curse of all mankind come to be upon the disbelievers? Will the disbelievers curse one another ? – it may be asked. The reply that some give to this is indeed that what is meant here is the curse of the people who are true believers. However, Allah has not made clear anything as to whether this cursing is in this world, or in the Hereafter, or in both places. At the same time, that in the Hereafter the disbelievers will deny one another and curse one another, Allah has, moreover, stated in Surah 'Ankabut 25. Here too, that may be what is meant. Allah knows. In any case, that for the disbelievers there is never any escape from the curse, that their experience will be punishment for all time, and that not even once will the punishment be lightened, or an interval of relief or a reprieve be given – Allah makes plain.

About the meanings contained in the word **إِلٰهُ** (Ilah = object of worship), in the commentary on Surat al-Fatihah, and about the meanings contained in the words **الرَّحْمَٰنُ الرَّحِيمُ** (Rahman, Rahim), in the explanation of the Bismi, it has been explained before. What is seen in the next verse are the proofs that Allah alone is worthy of being Ilah.

### Section – 20

*[Verse 164]*

*[p. 422]* The matters stated in this verse, each one, are plain signs of Allah's oneness, universal power, great favour, skill in creation and the like. However deeply and widely man is able to think about each one – or he becomes ready for it – to that extent will be the riches of knowledge that he attains from them. The signs that Allah has pointed out are these :

1) Creating the heavens and the earth: the infinitely vast heavens, the star worlds in them, unknown in number and in size, the planets, the satellites, the ordered and most wondrous movements of each one, the mutual relations, this earth on which we dwell, its motions, the continents it holds, the seas, the mountains, the rivers, the deserts, the lands, the forests, the living things, the substances, the differing landforms, the ways to subdue them and to exploit them – thus many and many things.

2)The difference of night and day: the two of them keep alternating, one after the other. If the time of one increases, the time of the other shrinks. When the day casts light on one half of the earth, on the other half the night covers it in darkness. Thus many things.

3) The ships that travel here and there through the seas carrying goods and resources useful to people. Or, without distinction of deep sea and distant sea, without distinction of night and day, in order to deliver the export goods of each land to other lands and import them, and to seek out and catch the resources of the sea, breaking through wind and storm and waves, like birds that go flitting and flying in the air, the countless water-vessels of various kinds that run back and forth, and the arranging of sea routes in a way suited to their travel, and so on.

4) Sending down rain water from the sky – from the upper part – and by that way giving new life to the barren lands lying lifeless. With the rain falling on land that lies dry, without moisture, without capacity to produce, its appearance changes; touched by wetness, the soil swells; plants, creepers and the like sprout up; it becomes fit for cultivation; thus the surface of the earth turns into a green canopy.

5)Distributing living creatures of various kinds on the earth. From organisms so small as not to be visible to the eye up to beings of giant form, *[p. 423]* from lower-rank beings that are thought to be the most undeveloped up to mankind, who are the most developed and of the upper rank, the countless living things that live on land and in the sea, in the countryside and in the forest, and in the air, their differences of kind-colour-class-nature, ways of life differing each from the other, needs, uses, shapes, natures and so on.

6) The management of the winds, and their control, the monsoon wind, the trade wind, the cold wind, the hot wind, the breeze, the gale, the whirlwind and the like, and their origin, course, benefit, harm and the like.

7) The management of the cloud that is controlled between the sky and the earth, the procedures of control of the rain clouds which, without rising upward more than needed, without coming down onto the earth in a harmful way and doing harm, stay confined in the air and keep moving on to the parts where needed, and so on.

That whoever it is who has been creating, controlling and managing all such great matters in an entirely ordered way, without their being contrary to or an obstruction to one another, only to Him – to Allah – belongs the worthiness to be Ilah (to be worshipped), and that all the other worshipped things are illusions that have no worthiness for worship, can be clearly understood by anyone who thinks using reason. Not only these; numerous signs like these besides.

However, for those who, having kept the real Creator out of the realm of thought beforehand indeed, out of hostility, and having installed in His place another creator – whatever it is said its name is, be it nature, or energy, or evolution, or explosion, all right – and worshipping it, think about them, it will not come about that they are able to discover, through them, the real Creator and His greatnesses. Prejudice and not using reason duly, and sometimes arrogance, will be the reason for it. But those who, without prejudice, use reason with the wish to behold the truth will discover the Creator and the greatness of creation, and will extol the Creator. In Surah Al 'Imran Allah says: In creating the heavens and the earth, and in night and day differing, surely there are many signs for the intelligent. That is, those who remember Allah standing and sitting, and on sides – lying down – and think about the creation of the heavens and the earth; continuing, Allah also points out that, as the fruit of their thought, they will say thus: **رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَذَا بَاطِلا سُبْحَانَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ،** *[DOUBT: this phrase (Al 'Imran 191) is transcribed as printed; on the 300 dpi crop no tanwin is visible on بَاطِلا, and the small marks are hard to read; the verse in data/quran-uthmani.json has بَٰطِلٗا.]* (Gist: Our Lord, You have not created all this meaninglessly. You are the Supremely Holy ! Therefore, guard and protect us from Hell! (Al 'Imran 190, 191)

*[Verses 165–167]*

*[p. 425]* That there are among mankind a kind of people who, although there are many signs such as were explained before, which even elementary intellects can grasp, without being ready to think about them, take some equals to Allah and keep worshipping them and supplicating to them; that the future they are to face will be extremely regrettable; that if they do not open their eyes now, they will open their eyes then; but that they will not get any benefit from that – Allah strongly warns.

About the intended meaning of the word **أَنْدَاد** (equals), it has been explained in the commentary on the 22nd verse. It can be seen that the commentators of the Quran have stated that what is intended here by equals is idols; that it is the leaders and chiefs of shirk; or else, that it is all things that are thought to have a likeness or a share in any of Allah's attributes, or works, or rights of authority. As many great men who are of subtle knowledge opine, *[p. 426]* it is the opinion stated last that is the most correct. It is, surely, the Arab mushriks that these Quran verses address in the first place. Then, it may be thought that it is the stones, trees, stars, planets, angels, devils and the like, to which they then ascribed divinity, indeed, that will be mainly what is meant. What Imam Baidawi(r) has opined is that all things that draw attention away from Allah may be included among the equals. As Sayyid Qutb has pointed out, in keeping with places and times, there will be changes in the nature of the equals too. People, or things, or emblems, or statues – all indeed may be reckoned as equals. How large the circle of ascribing equality to Allah is, and how hidden the ways for shirk to creep in are, can be understood by seekers of truth from quotations such as are seen below.

1. Ibn 'Abbas (r) says : ‘What Allah and you, sir, willed’( **مَا شَاءَ الله وَ شئت** ) – thus a man once said to the Prophet ﷺ. Then the noble Prophet said : You have made me an equal to Allah! What must be said is, ‘What Allah alone willed’ (A; Na; Ja. and others, and Bukhari in al-Adab al-Mufrad)

2. Hudaifat ibn al-Yaman (r) quotes as said by the Prophet ﷺ : Do not say, ‘What Allah and so-and-so willed’ ( **مَا شَاءَ الله وَشاء فلا ن** ). Say, ‘What Allah willed’, and, ‘Then what so-and-so willed’. (A; Da; Na; Ja; Ba; Ibn Abi Shaibah) There are other riwayahs too with this same meaning.

3. A riwayah that Ibn Abi Hatim (r) has quoted as stated by Ibn 'Abbas (r) is especially worthy of attention. It is as follows: What is called andad (equals) is shirk that is very hidden, like an ant creeping over a smooth black stone in the darkness of night (Addressing a person) saying thus: ‘By Allah indeed I swear, by your life too indeed I swear, by my life too indeed I swear (an oath)’; saying that, if so-and-so's dog had not been there, the thieves would have come to our house last night, and that, if there had not been the watchman, the thieves would have come; saying, ‘As Allah and you willed’; saying, ‘If Allah and so-and-so had not been there ....’. These are all shirk.

4. Ibn Mas'ud (r)asked the Prophet ﷺ which is the mightiest among sins. ‘While it is Allah who created you, it is your setting up some equal to Him’ – was what the Prophet ﷺ said in reply (Bu; Mu)

Keeping in front records like these, just consider some words that are current among the Muslim masses, and the beliefs those words represent! *[p. 427]* For example: If there is the help of Allah and of you, If the One who created and those to whom vows are made help, The protection of Allah and of the shuhada', With the help of Allah and of the ambiya' – awliya', With the barakah of the Shaikh – and many such! That all this is setting up equals of one kind or another to Allah can be understood by anyone who thinks with an impartial heart. The meaning of what was just said is not that, as soon as an individual says a word like this, one may pass judgement – must pass judgement – that he has become a mushrik, or that he has become an outcast from Islam. Because within shirk itself there is the manifest and the indirect, the small and the big, such a judgement may be passed about an individual only on account of clear disbelief ( **كَفربواح** ) that leaves no room for any kind of interpretation. It must be remembered that shirk is the biggest among sins, and one that Allah has clearly declared He will not forgive. That being so, however many reasons there may be in existence to justify it, all right, keeping entirely away from any matter that has the possibility of being among the kinds of shirk is indeed the indispensable duty of every true believer. Otherwise he must fear about his future.

When one sees some scholars toiling to keep apart from shirk, and to establish as Islamic practices, some matters such as calling upon and supplicating to some great men who are His creations just as one calls upon and supplicates to Allah, seeking help from them in the very same manner as one seeks help from Him, and carrying out vows and offerings in their name, one comes to feel great wonder and sorrow. Whatever interpretations and reasonings they may have to present, all right, or even if they embellish them by giving some good names, or by giving a religious tint, all right, in reality all of them are making equals to Allah in the direct sense indeed. For a person who is a seeker of truth and of impartial mind, there is no need to search much in order to understand the real state of such matters. Only two or three sentences of the Quran will be enough. For example : the sentence **إِيَّاكَ نَعۡبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسۡتَعِينُ** (To You only we do Ibadah; from You only we ask for help) in Surat al-Fatihah, which every Muslim keeps reciting daily, and the sentence **فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٗا** (Do not call upon and supplicate to anyone along with Allah) in Surat al-Jinn : 18, and the sentence **أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِكَافٍ عَبۡدَهُۥ** (Is Allah not enough for His servant ?!) in Surat-Zumar 36 – in reality, not even all of these three sentences are needed. Any one sentence among these indeed is enough – for a seeker of truth to understand the matter plainly.

One fact must be specially borne in mind. Since atheists *[p. 428]* are ones who deny Allah and His attributes, they will not be ones who make others equal to Allah. What they will do is install something else in the place of Allah. It will be those who are theists and, along with that, whose belief has not become right, who ascribe to others equality with Allah such people are the greater part of mankind. Allah says: **وَمَا يُؤۡمِنُ أَكۡثَرُهُم بِٱللَّهِ إِلَّا وَهُم مُّشۡرِكُونَ** (Most people among them – among mankind – do not believe in Allah except while being ones who practise shirk for themselves *[DOUBT: തങ്ങൾക്ക് (“for themselves” / “to them”) is printed so; its sense here is not certain: it may mean “while being, for their part, ones who practise shirk”.]* (Yusuf:106) The idol worshippers of former times and of this time too are not ones who deny Allah. They have belief in Allah. However, the reason for their shirk is that the belief is not in the real form and the complete form. That it is this very plight that has crept into the Muslim community too today – although it is with boundless grief and shame – one cannot but say. The very same pleas that the idol worshippers used to state to justify their worship. and keep stating, and some other pleas parallel to them, indeed, are what exist among the Muslims too to justify activities of a shirk nature. Although outwardly it is calling upon the great souls that one supplicates, in reality the supplication is to Allah ; knowing that only Allah in reality has the power to do good and harm, calling upon the great souls and petitioning for some matter is not thinking that they will bring that matter about for one. It is in the belief that, by means of their intercession, Allah will bring it about for one. Only petitioning with the belief that those great souls will bring it about for one on their own account indeed will be supplication..... – such and so on, surely, are the pleas usually stated for calling upon and supplicating to the great souls. Hearing such pleas that are heard from sections of scholars, ordinary people are also deceived! **نعوذ بالله**

For those who deliberately cover up the truth and misinterpret the authoritative texts, for selfish gains, or to maintain leadership and influence, or for party interests, there is no use in explaining records and proofs. Yet, for the sake of those who, through lack of knowledge and through misunderstanding, get caught in superstitions and wrong customs of a shirk nature, I wish to bring some points to attention here. Those who have good sense and purity of mind will surely be able to understand from it more or less the true state of things **إن شاء الله**

(1) Not a single Muslim has the claim that calling upon the anbiya', shaikhs, shuhada' and the like, who are great men and holy souls, and petitioning for help and for protection, is an obligatory rule in Islam, or that, if a Muslim does not call upon and supplicate to anyone other than Allah until his death, he will go astray from Islam. At the same time, that it is making those great men equal to Allah, and is shirk, is also something that becomes clear to anyone who examines with an open mind the Quran, and the Hadith, and the statements of the great men who were predecessors. That being the position, is it that, according to the claim of those who justify it, one may earn merit, or else, that, regarding it as shirk and the mightiest great sin, one must escape from the punishment of Hell, that a Muslim must decide ? Let those who have discerning intellect think !

(2) It was not believing that the gods that were their worshipped things were ones with the power to do good or harm to them that the Makkan mushriks used to worship them. It was with the belief that, by way of worshipping them, they would gain for them nearness with Allah, that they would make intercession for them with Allah, and that thus Allah would bring about their matter for them, that they had been worshipping. The plea of all idol worshippers who are not utter ignoramuses will be this indeed. Allah says: **وَالَّذِينَ اتَّخَذُوا مِنْ دُونِهِ أَوْلِيَاءَ....** *[DOUBT: the printed “....” stands at the left end of this Arabic phrase, so which side of it the dots belong to cannot be told from the page; they are placed after it here, where the omitted words of the verse fall.]* **إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلۡفَىٰٓ** (Gist: Those who have taken awliya' – guardians – besides Allah say: We do not worship them except in order to bring us near, a nearness, to Allah. (3) It is not that only bodily deeds like the prayer will be worship. Swearing an oath, making a vow, petition for matters that are to be obtained by an unseen way, or supplication, the belief that there is power for such matters – all these indeed are among acts of worship, and, if it is in the name of anyone at all who is not Allah, are shirk and making them equal to Allah. If any matters that are to be done in the name of Allah, and aiming at His greatness and pleasure, are done in the name of those who are not He, or aiming at their greatness and pleasure. that is making them equal to Allah indeed. May Allah give us all tawfiq to see the truth as truth and the untruth as untruth **آمين** *[DOUBT: the marks over the alif of آمين are not clear at 150 dpi; it may be printed امين or آمين.]*

Just as they love Allah, they *[p. 430]* will love those equals too ( **يُحِبُّونَهُمۡ كَحُبِّ ٱللَّهِ** ) – the gist of what was said is this: They do not deny Allah, or wholly forsake Him; however, what they do is, by way of offering acts of worship and supplications, honour them as they honour Allah, and show devotion to them as they show devotion to Him.
