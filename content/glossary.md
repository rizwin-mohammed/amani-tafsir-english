# Glossary of fixed renderings

Every translation session must use these renderings, so the same Malayalam word reads the same way in all 114 surahs. Only the reviewer (Rizwin) may change an entry. When an entry changes, every published surah that uses it is updated.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| കരുണാനിധി | al-Rahim (الرحيم) | the Ever-Merciful (literally "treasure of mercy") | Proposed, awaiting decision |
| രക്ഷിതാവ് | Rabb (رب) | Lord | Proposed |
